Weatherization Assistance Program (PA DCED)

Pennsylvania weatherization helps eligible households reduce energy waste and improve home comfort. Local providers deliver services in every county.

Who can apply

The current income guideline is 200% of federal poverty. Certain SSI, TANF, LIHEAP and qualifying housing-program households have an automatic eligibility route.

What help is available

Approved home energy improvements are services rather than a general-purpose cash payment. The provider determines appropriate measures.

Documents to prepare

  • Income or qualifying benefit evidence.
  • Household and property information.
  • Any owner or landlord permission the provider requests.

How to get started

  1. Find your provider

    Use the local-provider links on DCED Weatherization.

  2. Ask for intake

    Confirm the checklist and current scheduling.

  3. Arrange assessment

    Discuss property access, safety conditions and approved work.

After applying

Eligibility does not itself specify an installation date. Keep in touch with the local provider about the work plan.

Common questions

Is this the same as a heating grant?

No. Weatherization improves home energy efficiency.

Is there a local provider?

DCED lists providers covering all Pennsylvania counties.

Can benefits help establish eligibility?

Certain means-tested benefits provide an automatic eligibility route.

Who decides the work?

The provider assesses what the home needs under program rules.
