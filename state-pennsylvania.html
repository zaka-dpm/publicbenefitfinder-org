Assistance programs in Pennsylvania

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Pennsylvania WIC

    Pennsylvania WIC offers nutritious foods, nutrition services and breastfeeding support to eligible families. A local agency certifies participants.

    Read the application guide

  • SNAP Food Stamps (Pennsylvania)

    Pennsylvania SNAP provides grocery assistance to eligible households. DHS encourages people to apply even when they are unsure about eligibility.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • PALawHelp.org (Eviction & Legal Help)

    PALawHELP offers civil legal information and links to assistance in Pennsylvania. The Legal Aid Network provides a separate application route.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • LIHEAP (Home Energy Assistance)

    Pennsylvania LIHEAP helps eligible households with heating costs and qualifying heat emergencies. Intake follows seasonal announcements.

    Read the application guide

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (PA DCED)

    Pennsylvania weatherization helps eligible households reduce energy waste and improve home comfort. Local providers deliver services in every county.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • CHIP (Children's Health Insurance Program)

    Pennsylvania CHIP offers health insurance to uninsured children and teenagers who do not qualify for Medicaid.

    Read the application guide

  • Medical Assistance (Pennsylvania Medicaid)

    Pennsylvania Medical Assistance is the state’s Medicaid health-coverage program. The county assistance office reviews eligibility.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • TANF Cash Assistance

    Pennsylvania TANF helps eligible pregnant participants and families caring for dependent children meet basic needs.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Compensation (PA L&I)

    Pennsylvania UC provides temporary income support after qualifying job loss or reduced hours. The state evaluates wage history and separation circumstances.

    Read the application guide

  • Working Pennsylvanians Tax Credit

    The Working Pennsylvanians Tax Credit is a refundable state credit based on federal EITC. Claim it through income-tax filing.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • PA 211

    PA 211 helps residents find nearby assistance services through a free, confidential referral service.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
