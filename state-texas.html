Assistance programs in Texas

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • SNAP Food Benefits (Lone Star Card)

    Texas SNAP puts monthly grocery money on a Lone Star Card that works like a debit card at any store that accepts SNAP.

    Read the application guide

  • Texas WIC

    Healthy foods, breastfeeding support, and nutrition help for pregnant women, new mothers, and children under 5 in Texas.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • TexasLawHelp.org (Eviction & Legal Help)

    Free legal information, forms, and guides for Texans, run by the nonprofit Texas Legal Services Center.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • CEAP (Comprehensive Energy Assistance Program)

    Texas's LIHEAP utility assistance: helps low-income households pay electric, gas, and propane bills, including in a shutoff crisis.

    Read the application guide

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (TDHCA)

    Free home energy improvements for low-income Texans: insulation, caulking, duct work, and repair or replacement of inefficient heating and cooling systems, so future bills shrink.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Healthy Texas Women

    Free women's health services for uninsured Texas women 15 to 44: annual exams, family planning, and screenings and treatment for conditions like diabetes and high blood pressure.

    Read the application guide

  • Texas Medicaid & CHIP

    Free or low-cost health coverage for low-income Texas children, pregnant women, seniors, and people with disabilities.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • TWC Unemployment Benefits

    Temporary, partial income replacement if you lost your job or had hours cut through no fault of your own.

    Read the application guide

  • TANF Cash Help

    Monthly cash payments for very-low-income Texas families with children 18 and younger, paid on the Lone Star Card.

    Read the application guide

Child care assistance

  • Child Care Services (TWC Scholarships)

    Child care scholarships that pay most of the cost of care for low-income working Texas families, run by the Texas Workforce Commission through local Workforce Solutions offices.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • 2-1-1 Texas

    Texas's statewide helpline for finding local assistance of every kind: rent, utility bills, food, shelter, and healthcare.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
