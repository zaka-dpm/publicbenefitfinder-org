Washington 211

Washington 211 connects people with local health and social services. Use your location to find relevant providers.

Who can apply

The directory serves people seeking help in Washington. Each listed service sets its own eligibility.

What help is available

Referrals can identify food, housing, utility and other assistance. A listing does not approve benefits.

Documents to prepare

  • ZIP code or county.
  • Need, deadline and accessibility preferences.

How to get started

  1. Find help

    Dial 211 or 877-211-9274, or search Washington 211 resources.

  2. Explain the need

    Describe any urgent deadline and ask about suitable local services.

  3. Contact referrals

    Confirm intake, eligibility and availability directly.

After applying

Keep provider details. Ask for another referral if the first service cannot help.

Common questions

Does 211 award benefits?

Providers make their own decisions.

Can I search online?

Yes. The directory is available online.

What if 211 does not connect?

Use 877-211-9274.

Which documents are needed?

Ask the referred provider for its checklist.
