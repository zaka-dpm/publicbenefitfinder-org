Delaware WIC

Delaware WIC supports eligible pregnant participants, new mothers, infants and children younger than five. Start with a local clinic or WIC Client Portal.

Who can apply

Applicants must live in Delaware, meet income rules and have a nutritional risk assessed by WIC. Eligible categories include pregnancy, breastfeeding through the baby’s first birthday, non-breastfeeding postpartum through six months, and infants or children under five. Parents, guardians and foster parents can apply for children. US citizenship and marriage are not required.

What help is available

Support includes supplemental foods, nutrition education, breastfeeding help and referrals to health care.

Documents to prepare

  • Household income evidence, such as pay stubs, a recent tax return or employer letter; bring qualifying benefit evidence if applicable.
  • Identity and Delaware address evidence, such as photo ID and a utility bill, rent receipt or addressed business letter.
  • The baby or child under five, or pregnancy evidence from the doctor, as requested for the appointment.

Ask the clinic how to handle missing documents. The old DHSS dollar table is dated July 2024–June 2025; use the clinic’s current limits rather than that expired table.

How to get started

  1. Find a clinic

    Use the Delaware WIC clinic directory and request an appointment.

  2. Use the client portal if helpful

    The WIC Client Portal offers a new-client eligibility check. Existing DE WIC app users can use their credentials.

  3. Complete clinic screening

    Ask about records and who should attend. Clinic staff determine enrollment and explain benefits.

After applying

Keep clinic contact information and appointment dates. Ask staff for the current food and shopping instructions.

Common questions

Can existing app users use the portal?

Yes. The portal says to use existing DE WIC app credentials.

Is the portal check approval?

No. Complete the clinic’s eligibility process.

Who can help find a clinic?

Use the portal’s Clinics directory or contact Delaware WIC.

Is WIC only food assistance?

It also provides nutrition, breastfeeding support and health referrals.

Must an applicant be a US citizen?

No. Delaware’s applicant guidance says citizenship is not required.
