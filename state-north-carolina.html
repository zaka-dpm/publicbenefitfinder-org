Assistance programs in North Carolina

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Food and Nutrition Services (North Carolina SNAP)

    North Carolina Food and Nutrition Services provides grocery benefits through EBT. County DSS handles eligibility and interviews.

    Read the application guide

  • North Carolina WIC

    North Carolina WIC provides nutrition support for eligible pregnant or postpartum participants, infants and young children.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • Legal Aid of North Carolina

    Legal Aid of North Carolina offers civil legal assistance and a JusticeHub application portal. Intake does not guarantee representation.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Low Income Energy Assistance Program (North Carolina LIEAP)

    North Carolina LIEAP provides one annual vendor payment toward eligible heating costs. County DSS handles applications.

    Read the application guide

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (North Carolina)

    North Carolina weatherization improves home efficiency for eligible households. Apply to the designated local provider.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • NC Medicaid

    NC Medicaid provides health coverage for eligible residents. ePASS calls the program Medical Assistance.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • Unemployment Benefits (North Carolina)

    North Carolina unemployment benefits provide temporary payments to eligible workers. MyNCUIBenefits handles claims and weekly certifications.

    Read the application guide

  • Work First Family Assistance (North Carolina TANF)

    Work First is North Carolina’s TANF family-assistance program. It combines temporary support with employment services.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • Crisis Intervention Program (North Carolina CIP)

    North Carolina CIP assists qualifying households facing a health-related or life-threatening heating or cooling emergency.

    Read the application guide

  • NC 211

    NC211 provides free, confidential information about community health and human services. It is operated through United Way.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
