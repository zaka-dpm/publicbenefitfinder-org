Assistance programs in Montana

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Montana WIC

    Montana WIC provides nutrition support for eligible pregnant or postpartum participants, infants and young children.

    Read the application guide

  • Montana SNAP (Food Assistance)

    Montana SNAP helps eligible households buy food. Public Assistance offers online, telephone and local-office application routes.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • Montana Elderly Homeowner/Renter Credit

    Montana’s Elderly Homeowner/Renter Credit is refundable property-tax relief claimed through the income-tax system.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (Montana)

    Montana weatherization helps eligible households improve energy efficiency. The program uses a combined LIHEAP and Weatherization application.

    Read the application guide

  • Montana LIEAP (Low Income Energy Assistance Program)

    Montana LIHEAP helps eligible households with heating expenses. Heating-season applications run October1 through April30.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Montana Medicaid & Healthy Montana Kids (HMK) Plus

    Montana’s health application screens Medicaid and Healthy Montana Kids coverage. Public Assistance reviews eligibility for each applicant.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • Montana Earned Income Tax Credit

    Montana’s refundable Earned Income Tax Credit is claimed through Form2 with the state income-tax return.

    Read the application guide

  • Montana TANF (Cash Assistance)

    Montana TANF provides temporary financial help for eligible families with dependent children and certain pregnant applicants.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Insurance (Montana)

    Montana unemployment insurance provides temporary wage replacement for eligible workers. Claimant resources explain filing and continued payment requests.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • Montana 211

    Montana211 connects people with community resources through a statewide directory and telephone service.

    Read the application guide

  • Montana Legal Services Association (Montana Law Help)

    Montana Legal Services Association offers civil legal aid and public self-help resources. Online intake places applicants on a callback list.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
