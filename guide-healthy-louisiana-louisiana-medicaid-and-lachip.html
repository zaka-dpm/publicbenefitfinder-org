Healthy Louisiana (Louisiana Medicaid) & LaCHIP

Louisiana Medicaid includes coverage pathways for adults, children through LaCHIP, pregnant participants and other qualifying residents. Healthy Louisiana is how many members receive covered services.

Who can apply

['Eligibility depends on the coverage category, income and other conditions. Children can qualify even when a parent does not.', 'Adult expansion coverage is a separate pathway from coverage based on age, disability or long-term care needs. Tell Medicaid which circumstances apply to you.']

What help is available

Approved coverage can pay for doctor and hospital visits, medicines and other eligible care. Check the plan’s network and prescription coverage when choosing among available plans.

Documents to prepare

Prepare household identities, income and existing insurance information. The application lists the proof needed for your coverage category. Ask for the long-term-care application if that is the care you need.

How to get started

  1. Apply using MyMedicaid, call 888-342-6207, visit a Medicaid office or application center, or use the paper application linked from LDH’s Get Covered page.
  2. Submit all requested verification and keep your confirmation.
  3. After approval, follow the notice about Healthy Louisiana plan enrollment.

After applying

Keep your address current and respond to renewal notices. Carry both the Medicaid and plan cards when applicable. The current Healthy Louisiana notice describes work requirements beginning January 2027; read future instructions and do not treat them as a present application requirement.

Common questions

Can I apply outside an open-enrollment period?

Yes. Louisiana Medicaid accepts applications throughout the year.

Can I track a paper application online?

You can create a self-service account to check status and manage your case even if you applied another way.

Does selecting a health plan apply for Medicaid?

Coverage eligibility and plan enrollment are separate. Apply for Medicaid first, then follow enrollment instructions after approval.

Do announced 2027 work requirements apply now?

The current notice says the new requirements begin January 1, 2027, and affected members will receive instructions.
