Assistance programs in Minnesota

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Minnesota SNAP (Food Support)

    Minnesota SNAP adds money to an EBT account for groceries. Counties and Tribal Nations handle applications and interviews.

    Read the application guide

  • Minnesota WIC

    Minnesota WIC provides food and nutrition support for eligible pregnant participants, new mothers, infants and young children.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • Minnesota Legal Aid (Mid-Minnesota Legal Aid / LawHelpMN)

    LawHelpMN helps people find Minnesota legal information, clinics and legal aid referrals. Its guide asks questions to suggest suitable resources.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Minnesota Energy Assistance Program (EAP)

    Minnesota Energy Assistance helps eligible households with home-energy costs. The 2026–27 application deadline is May 31, 2027.

    Read the application guide

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (Minnesota)

    Minnesota weatherization helps qualifying households reduce home-energy use. Apply through the combined Energy Assistance and Weatherization process.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Medical Assistance (Medicaid) & MinnesotaCare

    Medical Assistance and MinnesotaCare provide health coverage for eligible Minnesota residents. The application route depends on age and care needs.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • Minnesota Family Investment Program (MFIP)

    MFIP combines cash and food assistance with employment support for eligible families with children and pregnant applicants.

    Read the application guide

  • Minnesota Child Tax Credit

    Minnesota’s refundable Child Tax Credit is claimed through the state income-tax return. Qualifying children must be younger than 18.

    Read the application guide

  • Minnesota Working Family Credit

    Minnesota’s Working Family Credit is a refundable income-tax credit for eligible workers with income below the applicable limit.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Insurance (Minnesota)

    Minnesota unemployment insurance provides temporary partial wage replacement for eligible workers who lose work or have substantially reduced hours.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • Minnesota 211 (United Way)

    Minnesota 211 connects callers with community resources around the clock. The service is free and confidential.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
