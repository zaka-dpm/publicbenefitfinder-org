New Mexico Child Tax Credit

New Mexico’s refundable Child Tax Credit is claimed on the state personal income-tax return. Use the PIT-1 packet for the tax year you are filing.

Who can apply

The credit uses the federal Internal Revenue Code’s qualifying-child definition and depends on family income. The state form determines eligible children and the applicable income band.

What help is available

A refundable credit can contribute to the return’s refund calculation. It is distinct from a monthly benefit application; amounts vary by tax year and income.

Documents to prepare

  • Year-specific federal and New Mexico tax records.
  • Dependent identity and qualifying-child information.
  • Income statements and filing-status details.
  • Any residence information requested by the return.

How to get started

  1. Find the correct PIT packet

    Use the state Child Tax Credit page and Forms & Publications link.

  2. Complete the child-credit calculation

    Follow the year-specific instructions for qualifying children and income bands.

  3. File the state return

    Submit required schedules and retain the calculation and confirmation.

After applying

Check refund correspondence and ask a preparer about disputed dependent claims. The state’s 2025 chart and January 2026 press release display different maximum amounts, so use the filed-year PIT instructions for the actual calculation.

Common questions

Is it a federal credit application?

No. This is a New Mexico return credit, although it uses the federal qualifying-child definition.

Can it affect a refund?

Yes. The state describes the credit as refundable.

Should I copy a dollar figure from an older article?

No. Use the tax-year PIT packet; published summaries can differ.
