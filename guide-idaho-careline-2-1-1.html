Idaho CareLine 2-1-1

Idaho CareLine is the state’s free community-resource referral service. Specialists answer Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Mountain Time; you can call or text for help locating services.

Who can apply

You do not need a public-benefit approval to ask for a referral. Eligibility for an actual service is determined by its provider.

What help is available

CareLine connects people with food, housing, healthcare and other community resources. It is operated by Idaho’s Department of Health and Welfare.

Documents to prepare

  • Your town, county or ZIP code.
  • A short description of the help needed.
  • Any urgent date and a way to record referral details.

How to get started

  1. Contact a specialist

    Dial 211 or 800-926-2588, or text 898211 during the published specialist hours.

  2. Explain your need

    Describe your location, immediate problem and providers already contacted.

  3. Follow the referral

    Ask the receiving provider about eligibility, available appointments and documents.

After applying

If a referral is unsuitable, ask for alternatives. Use the state CareLine page for current contact and online-search options.

Common questions

Does CareLine issue a benefit?

No. It provides information and referrals.

Who operates it?

The Idaho Department of Health and Welfare operates CareLine.

Is telephone help available all night?

The state publishes weekday specialist hours of 8 a.m.–6 p.m. Mountain Time.
