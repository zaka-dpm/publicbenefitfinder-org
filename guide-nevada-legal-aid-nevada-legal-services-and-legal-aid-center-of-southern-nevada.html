Nevada Legal Aid (Nevada Legal Services & Legal Aid Center of Southern Nevada)

Nevada Legal Services accepts requests for free civil legal help through online intake or local offices. Describe any court deadline when asking for assistance.

Who can apply

The organization screens income or grant eligibility, the issue and available resources. Services can include housing, public-benefit and consumer issues; not every request results in representation.

What help is available

Depending on the case, assistance may include advice, forms or representation. Southern Nevada residents can also explore Legal Aid Center services, but use that organization’s own current intake rules.

Documents to prepare

  • Court papers, eviction notices or benefit decisions.
  • A short timeline and the next hearing or response date.
  • Income and household information for screening.
  • Relevant leases, payment records or correspondence.

How to get started

  1. Start the NLS intake

    Use Nevada Legal Services’ online intake.

  2. Choose a local contact if needed

    Use the office directory for phone or in-person intake. Fridays and other office arrangements vary.

  3. Confirm the accepted service

    Ask whether the case is accepted and what you must do about deadlines while waiting.

After applying

Keep the intake confirmation and any instructions. Request another referral if the issue is outside the provider’s scope or it cannot accept the case.

Common questions

Can NLS help with a benefit termination?

Its published core services include SNAP, TANF and other benefit disputes, subject to intake screening.

Can I walk in at any office any weekday?

No. Office schedules differ, and some Friday visits require appointments. Check the directory.

Does submitting intake assign a lawyer?

No. The organization must confirm the assistance it will provide.
