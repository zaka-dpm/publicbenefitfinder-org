West Virginia WIC

West Virginia WIC provides nutrition counseling, breastfeeding support and food assistance for eligible pregnant or postpartum participants, infants and young children. Clinic certification includes a health and nutrition assessment.

Who can apply

['Applicants in a qualifying WIC category with a medical or dietary nutrition risk and income within program rules.', 'The clinic evaluates eligibility individually; benefit enrollment elsewhere can help document income but does not replace certification.']

What help is available

Specified nutritious foods and nutrition services rather than unrestricted cash. WIC uses a separate EBT card from SNAP and WV WORKS.

Documents to prepare

The state lists parent and child ID, address, income, pregnancy proof where relevant, child immunization records and custody or foster documents if applicable. Bring hospital discharge or crib records for an infant if appropriate.

How to get started

  1. Use Apply Now on the state WIC application page to begin contact.
  2. Arrange certification with the local clinic and confirm who must attend.
  3. Bring requested records and complete the health and nutrition assessment.

After applying

Ask the clinic how benefits are loaded and which shopping guide applies. Contact it for missed appointments, feeding or formula questions, a new baby, address changes or problems using food benefits.

Common questions

Is WIC cash assistance?

No; it provides food and nutrition services.

Can a Medicaid card document income?

The certification checklist lists Medicaid, TANF or SNAP benefit proof among income examples.

Do I need my child’s shot records?

The state lists immunization records for infants and children.

Can I use my SNAP card for WIC?

The state EBT office says WIC uses a separate card.

Can I ask about breastfeeding?

Breastfeeding support is part of the program’s services.
