Green Mountain Care (Vermont Medicaid & Dr. Dynasaur)

Vermont offers Medicaid coverage through Green Mountain Care, including coverage routes for children and adults. Eligibility categories have different application requirements.

Who can apply

Age, income, status and other circumstances determine the appropriate route. Older applicants, Medicare recipients and people with disabilities may need the aged, blind or disabled process.

What help is available

Approved coverage helps pay for covered health services. Review your program and plan information before arranging care.

Documents to prepare

  • Household income and identity information.
  • Requested status and current coverage records.
  • Resources and disability-related evidence if required for the category.

How to get started

  1. Choose the route

    Use DVHA Apply and the Vermont Health Connect links, or call 855-899-9600.

  2. Complete category-specific forms

    Ask whether supplemental aged, blind or disabled materials are needed.

  3. Read the decision

    Check effective dates and follow any plan or provider instructions.

After applying

Keep contact details current, review renewal mail and return requested information. Ask DVHA about appeal instructions if you disagree.

Common questions

Is every application identical?

No. Age, Medicare and disability can change the route.

Can I get application help?

DVHA lists telephone and local assister options.

Does submission activate coverage?

Confirm approval and dates in the decision.

Must I renew?

Respond to the renewal information sent for your case.
