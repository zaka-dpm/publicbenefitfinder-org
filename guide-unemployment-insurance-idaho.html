Unemployment Insurance (Idaho)

Idaho unemployment applications are filed through the Department of Labor Claimant Portal. The department does not accept claims by telephone; local Labor offices can help with online filing.

Who can apply

The agency reviews wage history, separation or reduced-hours circumstances and continuing weekly eligibility. Report your situation accurately, including earnings and availability for work.

What help is available

Approved claimants receive temporary payments through direct deposit or a benefit card. A new claim includes an unpaid waiting week that must still be certified and meet eligibility rules.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and Social Security information.
  • Employer, wage and separation records.
  • Work-search and weekly earnings records.
  • Bank information if choosing direct deposit.

How to get started

  1. Sign in and verify identity

    Start from the current filing instructions and follow the Claimant Portal’s ID.me process.

  2. Apply during the relevant week

    The state advises applying during the week you seek payment, rather than waiting until it has ended.

  3. Certify each week

    Complete weekly certification and the applicable work-search requirements, including for the waiting week.

After applying

Use Manage Claims to check payment status. Keep account and contact details current and respond to agency questions about wages or the separation.

Common questions

Can I file the claim over the phone?

No. Idaho’s filing page directs claims online, with local Labor-office assistance if needed.

Do I certify the waiting week?

Yes. The waiting week is unpaid but requires a valid certification and eligibility.

Where can I see a payment’s status?

The state directs claimants to Manage Claims in the Claimant Portal.
