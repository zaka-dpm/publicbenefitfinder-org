VA Benefits (Veterans)

VA offers several benefit programs for eligible veterans and, in some cases, dependents or survivors. Choose the benefit you need rather than treating VA benefits as one application.

Who can apply

['Eligibility varies by benefit and can depend on service history, disability, family relationship and other program rules. Accredited assistance can help identify the appropriate claim.']

What help is available

Benefits include disability compensation, education and training, and housing-related assistance. Each program has its own decision and application pathway.

Documents to prepare

Prepare service records and documents relevant to the requested benefit. For education, VA lists identity, banking, military or education history and school information. Ask about a dependent or survivor application when the benefit is based on another person’s service.

How to get started

Use VA’s benefit application directory. It links to disability, pension, education and other programs and explains alternatives to online filing. Ask a VA accredited representative for claim assistance if needed.

After applying

Track each claim and respond to requests from the relevant VA program. Read the decision before choosing any review or appeal route. Keep separate records for separate benefits.

Common questions

Is one application enough for every VA benefit?

No. Use the application for the particular benefit.

Can dependents or survivors apply?

Some programs cover dependents or survivors under their own requirements.

Does housing assistance mean a free home?

VA housing programs include loans and adaptation assistance; the specific program determines eligibility and terms.

Can someone help with the claim?

VA encourages assistance from accredited representatives.
