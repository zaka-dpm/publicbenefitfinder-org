Montana Medicaid & Healthy Montana Kids (HMK) Plus

Montana’s health application screens Medicaid and Healthy Montana Kids coverage. Public Assistance reviews eligibility for each applicant.

Who can apply

Age, income, household and program-category conditions matter. Explain pregnancy, disability or long-term-care needs.

What help is available

Approved applicants receive health coverage according to the program and decision notice.

Documents to prepare

  • Household and income details.
  • Existing insurance information.
  • Requested identity and eligibility records.

How to get started

  1. Choose the application

    Visit DPHHS health application guidance.

  2. Apply for multiple needs

    For health, food and cash together, use apply.mt.gov or call 888-706-1535.

  3. Complete verification

    Save confirmation and respond to requested information.

After applying

Read the coverage decision, follow renewal instructions and report changes.

Common questions

Can I apply for children separately?

List the people needing coverage so their eligibility is screened.

Can I apply for food help too?

The shared application route supports combined requests.

Does one denial determine everyone’s eligibility?

Different family members can have different coverage categories.

Is submission approval?

No. Public Assistance reviews the case.
