Florida KidCare

Florida KidCare uses one application to assess children for its health-coverage programs. The program matches eligible children to the appropriate option based on age, household circumstances and health needs.

Who can apply

Low-cost coverage is for eligible Florida children under 19 who meet residency, financial and status requirements. Working parents can apply; employment alone does not settle a child’s eligibility.

What help is available

KidCare includes Medicaid for Children, MediKids, Florida Healthy Kids and Children’s Medical Services coverage. Subsidized and full-pay options differ, so review the placement and cost notice.

Documents to prepare

  • Prepare each child’s date of birth and household information.
  • Have household earnings and current insurance details available.
  • Use KidCare’s requirements page for the accepted citizenship and identity records relevant to the child.

How to get started

  1. Create a Parent Portal account

    Apply at Florida KidCare. One application covers its program options.

  2. Use an alternative if needed

    A paper application is available. Call 1-888-540-5437 for application help or to request a copy.

  3. Complete the file

    Submit requested records and read the coverage placement and payment instructions.

After applying

Confirm when coverage starts and whether a premium is due. Keep the Parent Portal contact details current and read renewal messages.

Common questions

Do I apply separately to each KidCare program?

No. One application is used to determine the appropriate coverage option.

Can I apply during the year?

KidCare describes year-round enrollment.

Is Florida Healthy Kids the same as KidCare?

Florida Healthy Kids is one of the programs within the Florida KidCare umbrella.
