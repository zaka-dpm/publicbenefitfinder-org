Weatherization Assistance Program (Vermont)

Vermont weatherization improves home energy efficiency, safety and comfort through approved services. Regional agencies handle applications.

Who can apply

DCF lists no free weatherization within the prior 15 years plus an income or qualifying-benefit route. Active Fuel Assistance, SSI or qualifying adult Reach Up benefits can establish eligibility; child-only Reach Up does not.

What help is available

Approved services include a home assessment and energy improvements rather than unrestricted cash.

Documents to prepare

  • Agency application and signature.
  • Requested household income or benefit evidence.
  • Form 407 for each applicable wage job in the previous 12 months.

How to get started

  1. Find the regional agency

    Use the service-area links on DCF Weatherization.

  2. Complete agency forms

    Fill the application and required employee sections of employment-verification forms.

  3. Submit signed records

    Use the address on the application. Ask about extra forms if the home has separate living areas.

After applying

Discuss scheduling, property permission and assessment findings with the agency before work begins.

Common questions

Does child-only Reach Up automatically qualify?

DCF says that route does not count.

Where do I apply?

The regional weatherization agency listed by DCF.

Can I pick any renovation?

The agency provides approved energy services.

What if the building has separate apartments?

Ask the agency whether additional forms are needed.
