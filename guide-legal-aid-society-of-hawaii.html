Legal Aid Society of Hawaii

Legal Aid Society of Hawaiʻi provides free civil legal assistance to eligible low-income residents. Start with phone intake; the organization currently says it is not accepting walk-in intake.

Who can apply

Legal Aid assesses financial eligibility, the type of civil issue and whether it can assist. Tell intake staff about an imminent hearing or response deadline.

What help is available

Help may include legal advice or other civil legal services accepted by the organization. A request for intake does not itself confirm representation.

Documents to prepare

  • Keep court documents, notices and relevant agreements together.
  • Prepare a short timeline and the next deadline.
  • Have income and household details ready for intake screening.

How to get started

  1. Call the correct intake number

    Oʻahu callers can use 808-536-4302; neighbor-island callers can use 1-800-499-4302.

  2. Check the contact hours

    The current Legal Aid website lists weekday phone intake from 9–11:30 a.m. and 1–3:30 p.m.

  3. Confirm the assistance offered

    Explain your documents and deadline. Ask whether the case is accepted and what you must do while waiting.

After applying

Keep a record of the response and continue tracking deadlines. Request disability-related accommodation if the usual intake method is unsuitable.

Common questions

Can I walk into an office to apply?

The current website says physical offices are not open for walk-in intake; begin by phone.

Are accepted legal services charged to clients?

Legal Aid says its civil legal assistance to eligible clients is free.

Is intake the same as representation?

No. Ask the organization to confirm what assistance it has agreed to provide.
