Unemployment Insurance (Job Service North Dakota)

North Dakota unemployment claims start in UI ICE. File during the week unemployment begins; the agency says claims cannot be backdated.

Who can apply

Job Service reviews covered earnings and the reason work ended or hours decreased. Ongoing eligibility includes being able and available for work and meeting assigned work-search requirements.

What help is available

Approved benefits provide temporary payments for eligible weeks. An initial application alone does not request every weekly payment.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and employment information.
  • Employer contacts, work dates and separation details.
  • Weekly earnings and job-search records.

How to get started

  1. File the initial claim

    Use Job Service’s current filing instructions to reach UI ICE.

  2. Monitor claim tasks

    Check My To-Do List and respond to verification or eligibility requests.

  3. Certify weekly

    Submit each weekly request through UI ICE or the agency’s automated telephone route.

After applying

Report earnings and availability accurately. If you miss filing, contact Job Service about reopening and the affected weeks rather than assuming the account remains active.

Common questions

Can I wait and claim earlier weeks later?

The state says claims are effective for the filing week and cannot be backdated.

Is there a weekly telephone option?

The agency lists its automated system at 701-328-4995, using the required PIN.

Where do I find outstanding tasks?

UI ICE provides a My To-Do List for claim actions.
