Unemployment Insurance (Mississippi MDES)

Mississippi unemployment insurance provides temporary payments to eligible unemployed workers. MDES determines eligibility and processes weekly certifications.

Who can apply

Work history, separation and ongoing availability requirements affect the claim. Follow MDES’s current work-search instructions.

What help is available

A claim calculation and eligibility review determine payable benefits. Payments are not automatic after registration.

Documents to prepare

  • Recent employer and separation information.
  • Identity and contact information.
  • Weekly work, earnings and search records.

How to get started

  1. Start through MDES

    Use Unemployment Claims and its current online claim link.

  2. Complete the claim

    Answer accurately and read determinations.

  3. Certify weekly

    Submit each weekly certification and required work-search details.

After applying

Keep records and respond to notices. For filing help call 601-493-9427.

Common questions

Is registration enough for weekly payment?

No. Weekly certification is required.

Where do I find the current portal?

Follow MDES’s Unemployment Claims link.

Must I report work?

Answer the weekly questions accurately.

Who helps with filing?

MDES Contact Center at 601-493-9427.
