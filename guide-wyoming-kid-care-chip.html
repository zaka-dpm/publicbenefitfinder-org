Wyoming Kid Care CHIP

Wyoming Kid Care CHIP provides health coverage for eligible uninsured children and teens whose families do not qualify for Medicaid. The state accepts a shared Medicaid and CHIP application.

Who can apply

['Wyoming children and teens through age 18 meeting income and other CHIP requirements.', 'Applicants are assessed for Medicaid; tell customer service about existing insurance and status circumstances.']

What help is available

Health, vision, dental and prescription coverage. Preventive services are listed without copays, while other costs depend on the approved plan.

Documents to prepare

Prepare child identity, household income, residence and other insurance information. Keep any verification request and ask what alternatives are accepted for unavailable records.

How to get started

  1. Apply or renew through the state portal linked from the Kid Care CHIP page.
  2. For a telephone application call 855-294-2127.
  3. Paper applications can be returned using the current state application page’s instructions.
  4. Ask customer service to confirm the address before mailing if state pages disagree.

After applying

Respond to renewal texts and mailed forms. Complete and return the renewal by its deadline and update contact information promptly. Read the coverage decision, plan costs and participating providers. Enrollment and renewal do not require paying an application fee.

Common questions

Can I use one form for Medicaid and CHIP?

The state provides a shared health coverage application route.

Does CHIP cover adult parents?

Kid Care CHIP is a children’s coverage program; ask about adult options separately.

Are all visits free?

The state lists no copay for preventive care and plan-dependent costs for other services.

Can I renew online?

Yes; the CHIP page links the state portal.

What if my address changed?

Update it online or contact 855-294-2127 so renewal notices arrive.
