Michigan Home Heating Credit

Michigan’s Home Heating Credit helps qualifying homeowners and renters with heating expenses. The currently listed filing deadline was September 30, 2026.

Who can apply

Eligibility depends on occupying a Michigan homestead, household resources and other filing conditions. Use the correct claim-year instructions.

What help is available

The credit helps with heating costs and may be issued through an energy draft or direct payment arrangements. It is not an immediate shutoff application.

Documents to prepare

  • Annual household-resource records, not just monthly income.
  • Heating-cost records for the specified claim period.
  • Lease or property information, especially where heat is included in rent.

How to get started

  1. Check the claim year and deadline

    Review Treasury’s Home Heating Credit guidance. The listed September 30, 2026 deadline has passed.

  2. Use MI-1040CR-7

    The claim can be filed on its own if no MI-1040 return is required.

  3. Follow payment instructions

    If an energy draft cannot be used because heat is included in rent, follow Treasury’s review instructions.

After applying

Check claim status through Treasury eServices. For a current emergency, contact MDHHS rather than waiting for the next credit cycle.

Common questions

Can I file without a Michigan income-tax return?

Yes. MI-1040CR-7 can be submitted separately when no return is required.

Is the listed 2026 deadline still upcoming?

No. September 30, 2026 has passed.

Should I enter monthly income?

The instructions require annual household resources.

Where do I check status?

Use Treasury Individual Income Tax eServices.
