Food assistance

  • FoodShare Wisconsin (SNAP)

    FoodShare is Wisconsin’s SNAP grocery assistance program. Apply through ACCESS or the county or Tribal agency and complete the required interview.

  • Wisconsin WIC

    Wisconsin WIC provides food, nutrition education and breastfeeding support. Start a pre-application or contact the local WIC office, then complete its eligibility screening.
