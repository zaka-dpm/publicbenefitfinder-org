Work First New Jersey (Cash Assistance)

Work First New Jersey provides temporary cash and related support. Apply through MyNJHelps or your County Social Service Agency, then complete the interview and verification.

Who can apply

TANF serves qualifying families with children; General Assistance serves qualifying adults or couples without children. Income, resources, residence and status requirements apply, alongside case-specific work and child-support obligations.

What help is available

Approved cases receive monthly cash assistance and may receive employment, transportation or childcare support. The worker explains which category and obligations apply.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and requested status records.
  • Current income and resource evidence.
  • Residence information unless experiencing homelessness.
  • Children’s and caregiver details where relevant.

How to get started

  1. Submit the request

    Use MyNJHelps or send the state paper application to your county agency.

  2. Complete the interview

    Follow the phone or in-person appointment instructions. Describe an immediate lack of shelter, food or essential utilities.

  3. Provide missing proof

    Upload through MyNJHelps or follow the county’s instructions; apply even if some records are initially missing.

After applying

Read work-plan, reporting and time-limit notices. Ask about exceptions or hardship circumstances and how to document them before a participation problem occurs.

Common questions

Can an adult without children apply?

Yes. WFNJ includes General Assistance for qualifying individuals or couples without children.

Can I apply with an incomplete document set?

The state encourages applying; the worker then identifies missing proof.

What if I need help immediately?

Explain the immediate need at application so the county can assess that request.
