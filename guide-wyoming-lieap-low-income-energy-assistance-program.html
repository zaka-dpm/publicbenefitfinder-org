Wyoming LIEAP (Low Income Energy Assistance Program)

Wyoming LIEAP is accepting 2026–27 winter applications from October 1, 2026 through April 30, 2027. It helps eligible households with heating costs and separate heating emergencies.

Who can apply

['Wyoming households responsible for heating costs with income at or below 60% of state median income.', 'Owners, renters and residents of permanently parked RVs may apply.', 'Crisis applicants must document a recent qualifying energy payment under the rules effective October 1, 2026.']

What help is available

A contribution paid directly to the fuel vendor; seasonal benefits do not cover bills before October 1, 2026. Amounts depend on household circumstances and do not necessarily cover all costs.

Documents to prepare

Prepare household and income records, supplier accounts and current bills. For crisis help, keep a recent disconnection notice and proof of at least one utility payment within the past 60 days.

How to get started

  1. Use the application portal linked from the current LIEAP page; the updated short URL is MYLIEAPWYO.ORG.
  2. Submit a new application for this heating season.
  3. If unable to apply online, call 800-246-4221 for telephone help.
  4. For additional crisis help after seasonal application, call the LIEAP office to request it.

After applying

Check status and provide requested documents. Keep payment receipts and ask the vendor about pending assistance. WAP uses the joint application but has separate income and work-selection rules.

Common questions

Is the old LIEAP short URL current?

The state says the short URL changed to MYLIEAPWYO.ORG.

What is the current application window?

October 1, 2026–April 30, 2027.

Can benefits pay an old summer bill?

Seasonal benefits do not pay bills before October 1, 2026.

Is recent payment proof required for crisis aid?

The state’s October 2026 rule requires at least one verifiable payment toward a primary or secondary utility within 60 days.

Is weatherization available on the same form?

Yes, but WAP has separate requirements and year-round intake.
