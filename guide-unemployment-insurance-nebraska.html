Unemployment Insurance (Nebraska)

Nebraska unemployment claims are filed through NEworks. A weekly claim is required for each week you request benefits or waiting-week credit, even while an eligibility issue remains pending.

Who can apply

The Department of Labor reviews covered wages, job separation or reduced hours and continuing eligibility. NEworks registration and the applicable reemployment requirements are part of the process.

What help is available

Approved claimants receive temporary benefit payments. The amount and case status are shown in the NEworks claim summary.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and Social Security information.
  • Employment dates, wage details and employer contacts.
  • Records of reemployment activity and gross earnings for each claimed week.

How to get started

  1. Register and file

    Start with NDOL’s application instructions and access NEworks.

  2. Complete the claim review

    Answer the wage and separation questions and read requests for further information.

  3. Claim each week

    File for the preceding week during the published Sunday–Saturday window. Follow the reemployment instructions applicable to you.

After applying

Report gross earnings for the week in which the work was performed, even if paid later. Continue weekly filing while the agency resolves eligibility or an appeal.

Common questions

Do I file weekly while the initial claim is pending?

NDOL says yes, for each week you want payment or waiting-week credit.

Where do I see my award?

The agency points to the NEworks Unemployment Services Claim Summary.

When do I report earnings?

Report gross wages for the week the work was performed, not just the later payday.
