Alaska Medicaid & Denali KidCare

Alaska Medicaid covers eligible low-income residents through several categories, including children and adults. Denali KidCare is the children’s coverage pathway; the state checks the category and financial rules for each applicant.

Who can apply

['Potential eligibility groups include children, pregnancy, families, adults without dependent children ages 19–64, and older, blind or disabled residents. Income and other requirements differ by category.', 'A pre-screening result does not establish coverage. Use the current standards linked from the state application page and submit the application for a determination.']

What help is available

Approved coverage pays for eligible health services under the applicant’s category. Confirm that the provider accepts Alaska Medicaid and whether a service requires prior approval before receiving care.

Documents to prepare

Gather household and contact information, income records and current insurance details. Ask about additional medical or financial records if seeking disability-related or long-term-care coverage. Keep a copy of submitted records and any verification request.

How to get started

  1. Start at Alaska’s Apply for Medicaid page.
  2. Choose the linked HealthCare.gov application or Alaska Connect option. Answer the questions for each household member requesting coverage.
  3. Save confirmation and respond to DPA’s requests. Ask the worker about the correct application route for long-term-care needs.

After applying

Read the decision for effective dates and renewal instructions. Keep your mailing and contact information current. The state announces further changes beginning January 2027; follow the notices for your eligibility group rather than applying future rules prematurely.

Common questions

Can a working family qualify?

Yes. The state says many working families can qualify, depending on the category and income rules.

Can adults without children apply?

Alaska lists an adult expansion category for eligible people ages 19–64 without dependent children. Other requirements apply.

Are all applicants reviewed under the same rules?

No. Age, disability, pregnancy and family categories can have different financial and coverage requirements.

What should I check before an appointment?

Confirm the provider accepts Alaska Medicaid and ask whether the planned service needs approval.
