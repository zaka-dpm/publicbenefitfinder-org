Crisis Intervention Program (CIP) — Stokes County DSS

Stokes County CIP helps eligible households facing a heating or cooling emergency. Explain the health risk and urgent utility deadline when requesting an assessment.

Who can apply

Applicants must meet the income test, have an eligible citizen or noncitizen household member, and face a heating or cooling emergency. A past-due bill alone does not establish the full crisis criteria.

What help is available

The county publishes assistance up to $600 for a crisis, with total payments limited to $600 per state fiscal year, July–June. Approved payments go to the vendor; the amount depends on what is needed to resolve the crisis.

Documents to prepare

Have the utility bill, past-due or disconnection notice, household income and requested eligibility records ready. Explain the health or safety circumstances and date service may end.

How to get started

  1. Contact Stokes County DSS through its current service directory and request a CIP assessment. Identify any immediate heating or cooling danger.

  2. Complete the energy application and the worker’s income and crisis review. Ask which documents can be provided later if something is missing.

  3. Ask about the decision and vendor payment, then verify the arrangement with the utility. Do not assume intake pauses a disconnection.

After applying

Keep the decision, payment details and bills. If the approved amount does not resolve the problem, discuss the unpaid balance and other assistance promptly.

Common questions

Is it a general water-bill program?

Its purpose is heating and cooling emergencies. Ask DSS or a referral service about other expenses.

Can I receive more than one payment?

The county allows repeat assistance where eligible, subject to the $600 total fiscal-year cap.

Do I receive a check?

Approved CIP payments go directly to the vendor.

What if my notice date is close?

Tell DSS the exact deadline and health risk immediately, and contact the utility about available arrangements.
