Oklahoma Earned Income Tax Credit

Oklahoma’s earned income credit is claimed on the state income-tax return. Its calculation is tied to federal rules for 2020, so using today’s federal credit amount without the state worksheet can give the wrong result.

Who can apply

Use the instructions for the return’s tax year to assess earnings, filing status and qualifying children. Oklahoma Form 511-EIC provides the state calculation.

What help is available

For tax year 2025, the state instructions allow five percent of the credit calculated under the specified 2020 federal rules. Proration may apply when Oklahoma adjusted gross income is lower than federal adjusted gross income.

Documents to prepare

  • Federal return and income documents.
  • Qualifying-child and filing-status information.
  • Completed Oklahoma Form 511-EIC and any required Schedule 511-G.

How to get started

  1. Choose the correct year

    Use the Tax Commission’s filing page to obtain the appropriate forms.

  2. Complete the state worksheet

    Follow Form 511-EIC rather than copying a current federal figure.

  3. Claim on the return

    Transfer the calculation as instructed and include required supporting forms.

After applying

Keep the worksheet and return copies. Ask a qualified tax preparer about eligibility or a corrected return if the calculation is unclear.

Common questions

Why does the worksheet refer to 2020?

The 2025 instructions expressly use federal requirements in effect for the 2020 tax year.

Is there a separate benefit application?

The credit is claimed through the Oklahoma income-tax return.

When is Schedule 511-G relevant?

The instructions require proration when Oklahoma adjusted gross income is lower than federal adjusted gross income.
