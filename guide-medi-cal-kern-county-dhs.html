Medi-Cal — Kern County DHS

Kern County Human Services handles local Medi-Cal applications. The county assesses the coverage category and financial and other rules that apply to each person seeking health insurance.

Who can apply

['Eligibility varies by category and current state rules. Working or owning a home does not by itself establish that you cannot qualify; ask the county to assess your circumstances.']

What help is available

Medi-Cal offers free or low-cost health coverage for eligible people, including medical, dental and vision services. Some eligibility pathways can involve a share of cost.

Documents to prepare

Prepare identity, household and income information and follow the county’s case-specific verification request. Avoid treating the webpage’s long property checklist as a requirement for every applicant; ask which items apply to your category.

How to get started

Apply online at BenefitsCal or call 661-631-6807 to request an application. The county Medi-Cal page lists regional offices and phone, interview and mail alternatives.

After applying

Save the application confirmation and respond to county requests. Keep contact details current and read renewal notices. After approval, ask about your card, health plan and finding a participating provider.

Common questions

Can I apply without using the internet?

Yes. The county lists phone, office and mail options.

Must I attend an in-person interview?

The county lists phone and mail alternatives; follow your case instructions.

Where is the main county office?

100 East California Avenue, Bakersfield. Regional offices are listed on the county page.

Does applying also choose my doctor?

Eligibility and obtaining care are separate steps; ask about health plan and provider enrollment after approval.
