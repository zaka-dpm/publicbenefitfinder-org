Assistance programs in Arizona

Find Arizona programs for food, health care, utility bills and cash assistance, along with the agencies that run them.

Food

  • Nutrition Assistance (SNAP)Monthly grocery money on an EBT card
  • WICFood and formula for young children
  • School mealsFree and reduced-price breakfast and lunch

Health care

  • AHCCCS (Medicaid)Free or low-cost health coverage
  • KidsCareCoverage for children in working families

Utilities

  • LIHEAPHelp with heating and cooling bills
  • WeatherizationFree home repairs that lower energy bills

Cash help

  • Cash Assistance (TANF)Monthly cash for families with children
  • Unemployment insuranceWeekly payments if you lost your job

Who runs programs in Arizona

  • Arizona Department of Economic Security

    Nutrition Assistance (SNAP), Cash Assistance (TANF), LIHEAP, child care assistance

    des.az.gov

  • AHCCCS

    Arizona's Medicaid program, including KidsCare for children

    azahcccs.gov

  • Arizona Department of Health Services

    WIC for parents and young children

    azdhs.gov

Dial 211 to reach Arizona 211 for local food, housing and utility help, any time.
