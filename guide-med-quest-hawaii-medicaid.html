Med-QUEST (Hawaii Medicaid)

Hawaiʻi Med-QUEST accepts applications for Medicaid coverage online, by phone or using a paper form. Approved applicants receive health-plan enrollment instructions after the eligibility review.

Who can apply

Med-QUEST assesses Hawaiʻi residents under the applicable Medicaid categories and financial rules. Children, pregnancy, adult coverage, age or disability can involve different requirements. Apply for the people needing coverage rather than assuming the whole family receives one result.

What help is available

QUEST Integration provides health coverage through participating plans. Read the coverage and enrollment information to understand providers and benefits.

Documents to prepare

  • Prepare applicant details and household income information.
  • Have current insurance and relevant identity or status records available.
  • For disability or long-term-care needs, check Med-QUEST’s list of additional application forms.

How to get started

  1. Choose an application route

    Apply at Hawaiʻi’s medical-benefits portal, call 1-800-316-8005 or use the form on Med-QUEST’s application page.

  2. Complete follow-up requests

    Med-QUEST contacts applicants if more information is needed. Keep your phone and mailing details current.

  3. Choose a health plan

    If eligible, follow the enrollment-choice packet sent by mail and confirm your coverage start date.

After applying

Use the plan’s provider information before routine care. Contact Med-QUEST about an unclear determination or requested form; contact the health plan about coverage services.

Common questions

Can I apply by phone?

Yes. Med-QUEST lists 1-800-316-8005 as an application route.

Can I submit a paper application?

Yes. The application page identifies the form and the nearest eligibility office for mail or fax submission.

Are extra forms needed for disability or long-term care?

Med-QUEST lists additional forms for those circumstances. Check its application page before submitting.
