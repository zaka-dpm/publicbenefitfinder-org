Washington Legal Aid (CLEAR / Northwest Justice Project)

CLEAR and Washington LawHelp offer routes to civil legal assistance and self-help information. Intake does not guarantee representation.

Who can apply

CLEAR screens eligible low-income applicants for civil legal problems. County and issue affect the appropriate referral.

What help is available

Help may include advice, referral or representation. Washington LawHelp offers legal information and forms.

Documents to prepare

  • Court papers and notices with deadlines.
  • Relevant correspondence and income details for screening.

How to get started

  1. Identify the intake route

    Outside King County, call CLEAR at 888-201-1014, weekdays 9:15 a.m.–12:15 p.m. King County residents can start with 211.

  2. Explain the deadline

    State the issue, county and any hearing or response date.

  3. Use self-help resources

    Search Washington LawHelp while awaiting intake.

After applying

Keep seeking timely help if intake cannot accept the case. A pending request does not extend a court deadline.

Common questions

Is criminal defense handled here?

Use the appropriate criminal-defense or public-defense route.

Does intake promise a lawyer?

No. Screening and capacity affect assistance.

Why does county matter?

King County has a different initial referral route.

What should I state first?

Any imminent court or notice deadline.
