Assistance programs in Colorado

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Colorado SNAP (Food Assistance)

    Colorado SNAP provides food benefits through a Colorado Quest EBT card. Your county human services department reviews the application and determines eligibility.

    Read the application guide

  • Colorado WIC

    Colorado WIC provides food and nutrition support during pregnancy and early childhood. Start with a clinic call or the state’s online callback request.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Colorado LEAP (Low-income Energy Assistance Program)

    Colorado LEAP helps eligible households with part of their winter heating costs. The 2026–2027 application season begins November 1, 2026 and ends April 30, 2027.

    Read the application guide

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (Colorado)

    Colorado weatherization provides no-cost energy-efficiency work for qualified homes. A local provider evaluates eligibility and decides which improvements the home needs.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Health First Colorado (Medicaid) & CHP+

    Health First Colorado is Colorado’s Medicaid program. The application also helps identify other coverage options, including Child Health Plan Plus where appropriate.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • Colorado Child Tax Credit

    Colorado’s child tax credit and Family Affordability Tax Credit are separate state income-tax credits. Use the instructions for the tax year you are filing.

    Read the application guide

  • Colorado Earned Income Tax Credit

    Colorado’s earned income tax credit supports eligible workers through their state income-tax return. Additional state rules can cover some ITIN filers and certain younger workers.

    Read the application guide

  • Colorado Works (TANF Cash Assistance)

    Colorado Works is the state’s TANF program for eligible families. It combines cash assistance with case management, education, training and employment support.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Insurance (Colorado)

    Colorado unemployment insurance provides temporary wage replacement for qualifying workers. MyUI+ handles claims, follow-up tasks and payment requests.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • 211 Colorado

    211 Colorado connects residents with local food, housing, utility, child care and other assistance providers. Start with your ZIP code and the help you need.

    Read the application guide

  • Colorado Legal Services

    Colorado Legal Services offers civil legal help to eligible low-income residents and seniors. Its intake team checks the legal issue and available resources.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
