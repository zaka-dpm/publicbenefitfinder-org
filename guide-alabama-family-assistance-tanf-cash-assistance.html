Alabama Family Assistance (TANF Cash Assistance)

Alabama’s Family Assistance program provides cash assistance for qualifying families with children. County Department of Human Resources offices handle applications; OneAlabama offers an online route.

What help is available

Family Assistance is the state’s TANF cash-assistance program. The county evaluates the household before approving support; application submission alone does not establish a payment amount.

Documents to prepare

DHR’s checklist includes income and resource records, Social Security numbers for assistance-unit members, proof of relationship to the children and children’s birth certificates. Contact intake staff if you cannot obtain a requested record.

How to get started

  1. Use OneAlabama to apply online, or obtain the application through DHR’s eligibility page.
  2. For a paper application, mail or deliver it to the DHR office in your county of residence.
  3. Keep confirmation or a copy. Provide the verification requested by the county and discuss any accommodation needed to complete the process.

After applying

DHR’s FAQ says the county has up to 30 days to process the application. Contact that office about missing records or status. Read the decision carefully and ask about the required next steps, reporting duties and review options.

Common questions

Can I apply online?

Yes. DHR identifies the OneAlabama website and mobile app as Family Assistance application options.

Where should I send a paper application?

To the Department of Human Resources office in the county where your family lives.

What proves my relationship to a child?

DHR requires proof of relationship and children’s birth certificates. Ask the worker which records are suitable for your family.

Can I request help because of a disability?

Tell DHR about a physical, mental or learning condition that limits participation and ask about reasonable accommodation.
