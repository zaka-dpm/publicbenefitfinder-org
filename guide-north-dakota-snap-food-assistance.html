North Dakota SNAP (Food Assistance)

North Dakota SNAP applications use the Self-Service Portal or a paper application returned to the Customer Support Center or a Human Service Zone.

Who can apply

The state assesses household finances and SNAP eligibility conditions. Describe the people who purchase and prepare food together and answer the worker’s questions about household circumstances.

What help is available

Approved assistance is delivered through EBT for permitted food purchases.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and household information.
  • Current income and requested expenses.
  • Any verification items listed in your interview notice.

How to get started

  1. Start the application

    Use the state SNAP page to reach current application options.

  2. Complete the interview

    Keep your contact details accurate and answer requests for household evidence.

  3. Read the decision

    Review the certification period, EBT instructions and reporting duties.

After applying

Follow current work-rule notices and keep renewal dates from your case correspondence. Check state updates before relying on older purchase restrictions.

Common questions

Can someone help me apply?

Great Plains Food Bank lists SNAP application help at 855-405-0000.

Must I replace an older EBT card because its design changed?

The state says existing cards continue working after its June 2026 card redesign.

Was the healthy-choice restriction already active in October 2026?

The state reports requesting a November 2026 start; consult the current waiver page for implementation updates.
