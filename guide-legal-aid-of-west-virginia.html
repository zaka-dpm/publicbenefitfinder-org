Legal Aid of West Virginia

Legal Aid of West Virginia provides free help with accepted civil legal matters. Call directly when a hearing or filing deadline is within ten days instead of waiting for online intake.

Who can apply

['People with civil legal problems that meet Legal Aid’s eligibility and case acceptance rules.', 'Criminal matters are generally excluded. The intake page also identifies exclusions and alternative resources for foreclosure and many child support matters.']

What help is available

Advice or other legal services if the organization accepts the matter. Legal Aid does not charge clients, but court filing fees can still arise.

Documents to prepare

Collect notices, hearing dates, orders, the other party’s name and relevant correspondence. Put the earliest deadline at the top of your notes before calling.

How to get started

  1. Call 866-255-4370: Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday 8:30 a.m.–3:30 p.m.; Tuesday 8:30 a.m.–12:30 p.m.
  2. For a non-urgent matter, use the online intake screening if appropriate.
  3. Give a reachable telephone number and request an interpreter when needed.

After applying

Online applicants should watch for follow-up calls. Confirm whether the organization has accepted the case and what it will do. Keep following court instructions while seeking help.

Common questions

Can I apply online with a hearing next week?

The intake page directs callers with a deadline within ten days to telephone immediately.

Can I visit any office to begin?

The locations page says most people should use the application process first.

Is representation guaranteed?

Ask whether the matter has been accepted and the scope of help.

Are interpreters available?

Yes, request one during telephone intake.

Can it defend a criminal charge?

The intake page excludes criminal cases and directs people to other resources.
