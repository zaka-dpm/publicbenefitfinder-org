Township Trustee Emergency Assistance (Poor Relief)

For township assistance in Indiana, contact the trustee serving your home address. The Indiana Township Association provides tools to locate the correct office.

Who can apply

The local trustee reviews the assistance request and applicable standards. Start with your actual township; a nearby office may serve a different area. Ask about the type of help needed and current intake requirements.

What help is available

The association directs people seeking help with rent, utilities, food or medical bills to township trustees. The association’s membership or website help desk does not handle household benefit applications.

Documents to prepare

  • Your home address and a way to contact you.
  • The bill or notice showing the need and deadline.
  • Income, expense and household records requested by the trustee.

How to get started

  1. Locate your township

    Use Find My Township or Find My Trustee on the Indiana Township Association website.

  2. Contact the trustee

    Ask how to apply and whether an appointment is required.

  3. Explain the urgent need

    State the date on any notice and ask what evidence is required. Keep the application and decision.

After applying

Read the written decision and ask the trustee about any review or appeal process. If the office cannot help, use Indiana 211 to ask about other local resources.

Common questions

Do I apply to the association?

No. Its website directs assistance requests to your local township trustee.

How do I find the correct trustee?

Use the association’s township or trustee lookup for your address.

Will a trustee referral guarantee payment?

No. The office must assess the request under its standards.

What should I mention first?

Give your address, the help needed and the deadline on any urgent notice.
