Washington Basic Food (SNAP)

Basic Food is Washington’s SNAP program. It supplements an eligible household’s grocery budget.

Who can apply

Applicants must live in Washington and meet household-income and applicable status rules. Other requirements depend on the case.

What help is available

Approved food benefits help buy eligible groceries. The agency calculates the household amount.

Documents to prepare

  • Ask DSHS for the verification checklist.
  • Prepare household income and relevant expense records.

How to get started

  1. Apply

    Use Washington Connection, call 877-501-2233 or visit a Community Services Office.

  2. Complete the interview

    Explain household circumstances and an urgent shortage of food.

  3. Return evidence

    Meet the document deadline and read the decision.

After applying

Keep review notices and report changes as instructed. Contact local food resources while waiting.

Common questions

Is Basic Food SNAP?

Yes. Basic Food is the Washington program name.

Must I apply online?

Phone, office and mail routes are available.

Does submitting the form complete intake?

An interview and verification may still be needed.

Can an old income table rule me out?

Ask DSHS to assess the current household circumstances.
