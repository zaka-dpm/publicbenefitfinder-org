TANF Cash Assistance (Illinois)

Illinois TANF provides temporary cash and supportive services for eligible pregnant women and families with dependent children. Apply through IDHS.

Who can apply

Applicants must live in Illinois and meet the program’s financial and other rules. The state includes pregnancy or a child younger than 19; an 18-year-old child must attend high school full time. Applicants develop and follow a self-sufficiency plan.

What help is available

TANF can help with food, shelter, utilities and other basic expenses. Related services can support education, work and family stability.

Documents to prepare

  • Child, caregiver and household details.
  • Income and expense records.
  • Identification, residence and other case verification requested by IDHS.

How to get started

  1. Start the request

    Use ABE or the Family Community Resource Center serving your area.

  2. Return requested evidence

    Provide accurate household information and complete intake.

  3. Discuss your plan

    Ask about the work, training or support activities assigned to your case.

After applying

Eligible cash benefits are issued through Illinois Link. Follow the decision notice, case plan and reporting directions.

Common questions

Can I apply while homeless?

Yes. The state explicitly says homelessness does not by itself prevent TANF eligibility.

Can pregnancy qualify without another child?

The state includes qualifying pregnant applicants even without other children.

Is TANF the same as SNAP?

No. SNAP is food assistance with its own determination.

What if my child is 18?

The state requires an 18-year-old dependent child to be a full-time high school student.
