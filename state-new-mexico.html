Assistance programs in New Mexico

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • New Mexico SNAP (Food Assistance)

    New Mexico SNAP applications begin through YesNM or Income Support Division. HCA interviews applicants and requests records about household circumstances.

    Read the application guide

  • New Mexico WIC

    New Mexico WIC supports pregnancy, infant feeding and children under five. Request a clinic appointment to assess the family and explain food and nutrition services.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • New Mexico Legal Aid

    New Mexico Legal Aid accepts requests for civil legal help online and through its statewide intake line. Tell staff about any approaching court hearing or response deadline.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • NM Energy$mart Weatherization Program

    NM Energy$mart provides qualifying home-efficiency work through regional providers. Submit the household application to the provider serving your location, rather than to Hous

    Read the application guide

  • New Mexico LIHEAP (Energy Assistance)

    New Mexico LIHEAP helps eligible households with home-energy costs. Begin through YesNM or call the state public-inquiry line to discuss intake and an urgent utility problem.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • New Mexico Medicaid (Turquoise Care)

    Turquoise Care is New Mexico’s Medicaid managed-care program. Apply for Medicaid first, then follow the coverage and health-plan instructions in the eligibility decision.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • New Mexico Works (TANF Cash Assistance)

    New Mexico TANF is called NMWorks. Apply through YesNM or ask an Income Support Division office for assistance, then complete the eligibility interview.

    Read the application guide

  • New Mexico Child Tax Credit

    New Mexico’s refundable Child Tax Credit is claimed on the state personal income-tax return. Use the PIT-1 packet for the tax year you are filing.

    Read the application guide

  • New Mexico Working Families Tax Credit

    The Working Families Tax Credit is a refundable credit on New Mexico’s PIT-1 return. Complete the federal earned-income calculation and the state’s year-specific instructions.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Insurance (New Mexico)

    New Mexico unemployment uses the Workforce Solutions online claims system or its phone application route. The initial claim and weekly certifications are separate requirements

    Read the application guide
