Indiana Energy Assistance Program (EAP / LIHEAP)

Indiana EAP helps eligible households with heating and electricity costs. Local service providers process applications for IHCDA.

Who can apply

IHCDA uses household-size income limits based on 60% of state median income and reviews the most recent three months of income. Households whose utilities are included in rent may also qualify.

What help is available

EAP provides an annual energy benefit, generally paid to the supplier. It does not cover every energy bill. Explain any disconnection or depleted fuel supply promptly.

Documents to prepare

  • Income evidence.
  • Current utility statements.
  • A lease or tenant verification statement when utilities are included in rent.
  • Additional records requested for your circumstances.

How to get started

  1. Find your provider

    Use IHCDA’s county lookup.

  2. Apply for the current season

    The 2026–2027 window runs October 1, 2026 through April 30, 2027. Apply online through LITT, or arrange mail or in-person intake with your provider.

  3. Save your login details

    LITT replaces EAPConnect. Retain its login number, code and submission confirmation.

After applying

Continue making payments while the request is reviewed. For a shutoff, contact both the utility and provider. Follow the written appeal instructions if denied.

Common questions

Is EAPConnect still used?

No. IHCDA has switched online applications to LITT for program year 2027.

Can renters apply?

Yes; the program also considers qualifying households with utilities included in rent.

Will the benefit cover the whole year?

No. EAP supplements household energy costs.

Where do I report an urgent shutoff?

Tell your local provider immediately and contact the utility about the notice.
