NC Medicaid

NC Medicaid provides health coverage for eligible residents. ePASS calls the program Medical Assistance.

  1. Who can apply

Who can apply

Income, age, household and care needs affect eligibility. Older, blind, disabled or long-term-care applicants may need additional forms.

What help is available

Approved coverage helps pay eligible health-care costs. Read the notice for coverage and enrollment instructions.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity, residency and household details.
  • Income and insurance records.
  • Requested eligibility or care information.

How to get started

  1. Apply

    Use Medicaid application guidance: ePASS, county DSS or HealthCare.gov.

  2. Identify your needs

    Tell DSS about disability, older-age or long-term-care circumstances.

  3. Respond to requests

    Check mail and account notices and send proof.

After applying

Keep contact information current and complete renewal steps.

Common questions

Can I apply anytime?

Yes, throughout the year.

What is the name in ePASS?

Medical Assistance.

Should I apply if unsure?

The state recommends applying for a decision.

Can I get language help?

Ask county DSS for an interpreter.
