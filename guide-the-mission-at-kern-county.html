The Mission at Kern County

The Mission at Kern County provides meals and services in Bakersfield, with overnight shelter described for men. Meal access and shelter intake are separate questions.

What help is available

Services include meals, clothing and support referrals. Residents can access hygiene facilities and other listed support. Program access and schedules should be confirmed with staff.

Documents to prepare

For a meal, ask the information booth where to go. For shelter, explain your circumstances and ask about intake, identification, arrival time and belongings; do not assume a meal visit reserves a bed.

How to get started

Check in at the information booth at 816 East 21st Street, Bakersfield, as directed by the Mission’s homeless services page. Ask staff for current meal and shelter instructions.

After applying

Clarify the next visit or overnight requirements and ask about housing and benefits referrals. If the shelter cannot serve your household, contact 211 for another placement option.

Common questions

Can someone outside the shelter receive a meal?

The provider says anyone in the community can receive a hot meal.

Is the overnight shelter described for all households?

The inspected page describes men’s overnight shelter. Ask about alternatives for other households.

Does a meal guarantee a bed?

No. Ask separately about shelter intake and available space.

Why are exact dinner hours not listed here?

The provider’s dinner time contains a typographical error; confirm the current schedule with staff.
