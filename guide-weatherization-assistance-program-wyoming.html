Weatherization Assistance Program (Wyoming)

Wyoming weatherization offers free energy improvements for eligible households. Applications are accepted year-round through the joint LIEAP and WAP application, with work selected by priority and a home energy audit.

Who can apply

['Wyoming households meeting the weatherization income rules, normally up to 200% of federal poverty guidelines.', 'SNAP, SSI or TANF participation can establish income eligibility.', 'Owners and renters can apply; renters need written landlord permission.']

What help is available

Approved efficiency and safety measures such as insulation, air sealing or heating system work. The agency decides appropriate work after assessing the home.

Documents to prepare

Prepare household income and address information and property details. Include the required rental agreement if renting and make sure adults sign the application as directed.

How to get started

  1. Use the application portal linked on the current state WAP page.
  2. If seeking only weatherization, use the joint application and select the WAP box.
  3. Respond to requested proof and arrange the energy audit if selected.
  4. For help call 800-246-4221.

After applying

Approval does not guarantee services that year because selection is prioritized. Confirm audit and work appointments. A property generally cannot be weatherized again until 15 years after prior service. Contact the weatherization agency first with a work complaint.

Common questions

Can I apply outside winter?

Yes, WAP accepts applications year-round.

Can a mobile home qualify?

The state lists mobile homes among eligible property types.

Does LIEAP approval guarantee work?

No; it establishes consideration, and services use a priority system.

Can a renter apply?

Yes, with written landlord permission and required rental paperwork.

Can the home be weatherized every year?

No; the state generally requires 15 years between services.
