California Courts Self-Help (Eviction)

California Courts’ eviction self-help guide explains tenant responses, court forms and the steps in an unlawful-detainer case. It does not provide rent payments.

Who can apply

Tenants facing eviction can use the guide. The required response depends on whether they received a landlord’s notice or a filed court Summons and Complaint, and how court papers were served.

What help is available

The guide explains response forms, filing, serving the landlord and the court process. A court self-help center can help with procedure; legal aid may assess advice or representation.

Documents to prepare

Keep the notice, Summons, Complaint, envelopes, service dates, lease, rent records and related communications. Ask a court self-help center to confirm the response deadline.

How to get started

  1. Open the tenant Answer instructions. Do not use an old five-day deadline from a historical screenshot: current instructions use ten court days for personal service, with different counting for other service methods.

  2. Complete and file the appropriate response and arrange required service. Ask about a fee waiver if you cannot afford filing fees.

After applying

Keep court-stamped copies and watch for hearing information. Contact legal help promptly; starting an online form alone does not file it with the court.

Common questions

Does this resource pay overdue rent?

No. It explains eviction procedure and forms.

Is a landlord’s notice the same as a court lawsuit?

No. Identify the papers before choosing your response.

Does filling a form online submit it?

Not necessarily. Follow the court’s filing and service instructions.

Is the response deadline identical for every service method?

No. Use the current tenant guide and confirm the calculation with court self-help.
