Weatherization Assistance Program (Maryland)

Maryland weatherization helps income-eligible owners and renters with approved home energy improvements. A local weatherization agency handles intake and the energy assessment.

Who can apply

What help is available

The assessment can identify air sealing, insulation, heating, hot-water, lighting and safety measures. The dwelling’s condition and program rules determine the approved work.

Documents to prepare

Prepare income records or qualifying-benefit proof and information about ownership, tenancy and utility use. Ask the local agency for its checklist and any landlord permission requirements.

How to get started

  1. Use the local-agency directory on DHCD’s weatherization page.
  2. Submit the agency’s application; benefit participation does not remove this step.
  3. After eligibility review, arrange the energy inspection and discuss the recommended work.

After applying

Ask about priorities and the waiting list. Confirm the approved scope and final inspection. If a property condition prevents work, ask what must be corrected and whether another repair program can help.

Common questions

Do renters qualify?

Yes. Contact the local agency about the rental property and owner consent.

Does Medicaid automatically establish income eligibility?

No. Maryland’s WAP page specifically lists Medicaid among programs not accepted for automatic income eligibility.

Does receiving SNAP mean no application is needed?

No. It can establish income eligibility, but a weatherization application still must be submitted.

Can I choose every improvement on the state’s list?

No. An energy inspection and dwelling conditions determine appropriate approved measures.
