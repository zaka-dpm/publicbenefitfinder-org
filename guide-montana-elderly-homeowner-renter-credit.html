Montana Elderly Homeowner/Renter Credit

Montana’s Elderly Homeowner/Renter Credit is refundable property-tax relief claimed through the income-tax system.

  2. What help is available
  3. Documents to prepare

Who can apply

Applicants must be62+ at year-end, meet Montana and home-occupancy duration rules and pass the total-household-income test.

What help is available

The amount depends on household income, rent and property tax. Renters and homeowners can qualify.

Documents to prepare

  • Income for all household members.
  • Rent or property-tax records.
  • Age and occupancy information.

How to get started

  1. Check eligibility

    Use Revenue’s credit page for the claim year.

  2. Choose filing method

    Use Form2 and Schedule2EC; eligible people without another filing requirement can use TAP’s credit route.

  3. Submit and save

    Retain calculations and the filing confirmation.

After applying

A person without a normal income-tax filing requirement still needs to claim the credit.

Common questions

Can renters qualify?

Yes.

Is it refundable?

Yes.

Does only my income count?

The rule uses total household income.

Can nonfilers claim it?

Eligible people can use the designated credit filing route.
