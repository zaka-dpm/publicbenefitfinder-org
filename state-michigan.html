Assistance programs in Michigan

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Food Assistance Program (Michigan SNAP)

    Michigan’s Food Assistance Program is SNAP. Apply and manage the case through MI Bridges; approved benefits use the Bridge Card.

    Read the application guide

  • Michigan WIC

    Michigan WIC supports eligible pregnant, postpartum or breastfeeding participants, infants and children younger than five. Local agencies complete enrollment.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (Michigan)

    Michigan weatherization provides no-cost energy-conservation services for qualifying households. Local Community Action Agencies handle applications.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Michigan Medicaid & Healthy Michigan Plan

    Michigan offers several health-coverage pathways, including Medicaid and Healthy Michigan Plan. MI Bridges or the current paper application starts the assessment.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • Family Independence Program (Michigan Cash Assistance)

    Michigan’s Family Independence Program provides temporary cash assistance to eligible pregnant applicants and families with minor children. Apply through MI Bridges or MDHHS.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Benefits (Michigan)

    Michigan unemployment claims use MiWAM through MiLogin. UIA checks the initial claim and continuing certifications.

    Read the application guide

  • Michigan Earned Income Tax Credit

    Michigan EITC is a refundable income-tax credit for eligible workers. Claim it on MI-1040 after claiming federal EITC.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • Michigan 211

    Michigan 211 offers free, confidential referrals to community assistance. Phone support operates 24 hours a day, every day.

    Read the application guide

  • Michigan Legal Help

    Michigan Legal Help provides free self-help information and tools for Michigan legal issues. Its Guide to Legal Help can identify lawyer referrals.

    Read the application guide

  • State Emergency Relief (Michigan SER)

    State Emergency Relief addresses qualifying Michigan emergencies involving housing, utilities, essential repairs or burial costs. It is intended for occasional crises.

    Read the application guide

  • Michigan Home Heating Credit

    Michigan’s Home Heating Credit helps qualifying homeowners and renters with heating expenses. The currently listed filing deadline was September 30, 2026.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
