Washington TANF / WorkFirst (Cash Assistance)

Washington TANF provides temporary cash support for eligible families. WorkFirst helps participants prepare for employment.

  1. Who can apply

Who can apply

Eligible circumstances include pregnancy or caring for a minor child. Income, resources and time limits apply; some children remain eligible while in qualifying school through age 18.

What help is available

Approved cash assistance supports family needs. A non-needy caregiver route may support eligible children.

Documents to prepare

  • Household, pregnancy or child details.
  • Income and resource evidence requested by DSHS.

How to get started

  1. Apply

    Use Washington Connection or contact a Community Services Office.

  2. Complete the interview

    Call 877-501-2233 or attend in person, weekdays 8 a.m.–2 p.m.

  3. Discuss WorkFirst

    Review participation requirements and any barriers with the caseworker.

After applying

Follow the assigned plan and review notices. Ask about an exemption or accommodation rather than missing a required activity.

Common questions

Can a relative caregiver apply?

DSHS lists routes for children living with relative or other caregivers.

Is cash ongoing without review?

Eligibility and time limits still apply.

Where is the online form?

Washington Connection.

What if participation is difficult?

Discuss the barrier with the caseworker.
