Virginia WIC

Virginia WIC provides food benefits, nutrition education and breastfeeding support. A local clinic reviews eligibility and nutritional needs.

Who can apply

Applicants must meet participant-category, residence, income and nutrition-risk rules. Qualifying groups include pregnant and postpartum participants, infants and children younger than five.

What help is available

Assigned nutritious foods are purchased with eWIC at authorized stores. Counseling and referrals are also available.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and address records.
  • Income or benefit evidence requested by the clinic.
  • Pregnancy or child information for assessment.

How to get started

  1. Start intake

    Use Apply Now on Virginia WIC, which links to myvawic.org.

  2. Arrange certification

    Ask the clinic what records to bring and who must attend.

  3. Review the package

    Staff explain the approved foods, card use and follow-up needs.

After applying

Keep appointments and check the benefit period before shopping. An initial online request is not a completed eligibility decision.

Common questions

Can I use WIC at any shop?

Use an authorized WIC store.

Are all grocery items covered?

Only assigned eligible foods are covered.

Is online intake final approval?

The clinic must complete the eligibility review.

Does WIC provide breastfeeding help?

Yes. It includes breastfeeding promotion and support.
