Assistance programs in Washington

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Washington Basic Food (SNAP)

    Basic Food is Washington's name for SNAP: monthly food money on an EBT card you use like a debit card at most grocery stores.

    Read the application guide

  • Washington WIC

    WIC gives healthy food, breastfeeding support, nutrition education, and health referrals to pregnant and postpartum people, infants, and children up to age 5.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • Washington Legal Aid (CLEAR / Northwest Justice Project)

    Washington Legal Aid (CLEAR / Northwest Justice Project): how to apply, documents to prepare and common questions.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (Washington)

    Free home energy-efficiency work for income-eligible Washington households: insulation, air sealing, ventilation, and related repairs that lower future bills.

    Read the application guide

  • Washington Energy Assistance (LIHEAP)

    LIHEAP helps low-income Washington households pay home heating and energy bills.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Washington Apple Health (Medicaid)

    Washington Apple Health (Medicaid): how to apply, documents to prepare and common questions.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • Aged, Blind, or Disabled (ABD) Cash Assistance

    ABD is a Washington cash program from DSHS for low-income adults who are 65 or older, blind, or likely to meet the Social Security disability standard.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Benefits (Washington ESD)

    Temporary weekly payments if you lost work through no fault of your own, from the Washington Employment Security Department.

    Read the application guide

  • Washington TANF / WorkFirst (Cash Assistance)

    Temporary Assistance for Needy Families, TANF, is Washington's monthly cash assistance for low-income families with children.

    Read the application guide

  • Washington Working Families Tax Credit

    The Working Families Tax Credit is Washington's refundable cash credit, run by the Department of Revenue.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • Washington 211

    Dial 2-1-1 from any Washington phone, or call 1-877-211-9274, for free, confidential help finding local assistance with housing, utilities, food, healthcare, and more.

    Read the application guide
