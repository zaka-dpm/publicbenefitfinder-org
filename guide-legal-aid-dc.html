Legal Aid DC

Legal Aid DC offers free help for eligible people with legal matters in the District. Apply online, by phone or through a listed intake location.

Who can apply

The program generally serves low-income households with issues in its practice areas. A DC case may qualify even if the person lives elsewhere; resources limit acceptance.

What help is available

Help can concern housing, benefits, family matters, debt, immigration or record sealing. Intake does not guarantee representation.

Documents to prepare

  • Notices, summons and deadlines.
  • Household income details.
  • Relevant records; you can submit online intake even without documents to upload.

How to get started

  1. Apply for help

    Use the online form linked from Legal Aid DC, or call 202-628-1161.

  2. Describe urgent dates

    State any hearing, eviction or benefit-response deadline clearly.

  3. Wait for the intake response

    Legal Aid reviews online submissions and contacts applicants when it may help. The page says there is no need to call again merely because you applied online.

After applying

Check email and phone messages. Confirm current office locations before a visit; the Anacostia office has moved.

Common questions

Can I apply without uploading papers?

Yes. The online application can still be submitted.

Should I call again after online intake?

The page says that is not necessary.

Must I live in DC?

A legal matter in DC may qualify even if you live elsewhere.

Is acceptance guaranteed?

No. Eligibility and resources affect the decision.
