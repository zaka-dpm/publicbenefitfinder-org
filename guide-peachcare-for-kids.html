PeachCare for Kids

PeachCare for Kids is assessed through Georgia’s Medical Assistance application. The state first determines which child-coverage program fits your household; you do not need a separate PeachCare application.

Who can apply

Children must meet PeachCare’s age, residence, financial, status and other eligibility rules. A child qualifying under Medicaid’s rules is placed in the appropriate Medicaid category instead.

What help is available

Coverage includes preventive and sick care, hospital services, prescriptions, dental, vision and mental-health care. Read the plan and cost notice for your child.

Documents to prepare

  • Prepare each child’s identifying details and household composition.
  • Have household income and existing insurance information available.
  • Provide the child’s identity or status evidence when requested.

How to get started

  1. Select Medical Assistance

    Apply through Georgia Gateway. The state also lists phone, mail and local DFCS or RSM office routes.

  2. Complete the child’s application

    Describe current coverage and requested household circumstances. Call ahead before visiting an office.

  3. Review placement and costs

    Read whether the child was approved for Medicaid or PeachCare and follow any payment and plan-selection instructions.

After applying

Check Gateway notices about renewal, premiums or changes. Confirm the child’s primary-care provider and covered services with the plan.

Common questions

Do I need two applications for Medicaid and PeachCare?

No. One Medical Assistance application is used to assess the appropriate coverage.

How can I get application help?

The application page lists 877-427-3224 or 877-423-4746 and local office options.

Does PeachCare include dental and vision care?

The state’s benefit page includes dental and vision services; ask the plan about participating providers.
