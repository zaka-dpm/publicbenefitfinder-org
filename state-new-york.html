Assistance programs in New York

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • New York WIC

    New York WIC provides food and nutrition support to eligible pregnant or postpartum participants, infants and children younger thanfive.

    Read the application guide

  • SNAP Food Benefits (New York)

    New York SNAP helps eligible households buy food through EBT. Application routes differ between New York City and other counties.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • LawHelpNY.org (Eviction & Legal Help)

    LawHelpNY offers free legal information and a directory of civil legal aid providers. Search by location and legal issue.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • HEAP (Home Energy Assistance Program)

    New York HEAP helps eligible households with heating costs. Regular, emergency and equipment assistance have separate intake rules and funding windows.

    Read the application guide

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (NYS HCR)

    New York weatherization improves efficiency in eligible homes through local providers. Renters and homeowners can apply.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Child Health Plus

    Child Health Plus provides health coverage for eligible New York children younger than19 who are not eligible for Medicaid.

    Read the application guide

  • New York Medicaid

    New York Medicaid application routes depend on age and eligibility category. The marketplace and local districts serve different applicants.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • New York State Earned Income Credit

    New York’s earned income credit is claimed on the state income-tax return. It is linked to the same-year federal EITC.

    Read the application guide

  • Temporary Assistance (Family Assistance & Safety Net)

    New York Temporary Assistance includes Family Assistance and Safety Net Assistance. Local districts assess regular and emergency requests.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Insurance (NYS DOL)

    New York unemployment insurance provides temporary payments to eligible workers. Initial claims and weekly certifications are separate.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • 2-1-1 New York

    211 New York connects people with local services through regional providers covering the state around the clock.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
