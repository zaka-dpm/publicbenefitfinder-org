Weatherization Assistance Program (Iowa)

Iowa weatherization helps eligible households improve home energy efficiency. Applications go through the community action agency serving the county.

Who can apply

The program serves low-income households, with particular attention to older adults, people with disabilities and children. Ask the county agency about current screening and property requirements.

What help is available

An energy audit determines cost-effective work. Measures can include insulation, air sealing and combustion-appliance safety checks.

Documents to prepare

Ask the agency which income and residence records it needs, and whether owner permission or property information is necessary. Do not send account credentials or original ownership papers without instructions.

How to get started

  1. Find the county’s community action office through the state’s linked agency directory. If you cannot locate it, call the Community Action Agencies Unit at 515-776-8871.

  2. Complete intake and arrange the assessment if selected. Ask which work is approved and when access to the home is needed.

After applying

Keep assessment and contractor paperwork. For heating-bill assistance, ask separately about LIHEAP; weatherization concerns improvements to the home.

Common questions

Who decides which improvements are made?

The program’s energy audit identifies suitable, cost-effective measures.

Will the program pay my utility bill?

This program funds approved home efficiency work; ask about LIHEAP for bill assistance.

Where do I apply?

At the community action agency serving your Iowa county.

Can I pick any renovation?

Ask the agency about approved measures. The audit guides the work.
