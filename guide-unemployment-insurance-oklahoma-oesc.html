Unemployment Insurance (Oklahoma OESC)

Oklahoma unemployment uses the OESC claimant portal for initial claims, identity verification and weekly certifications. An application and a weekly payment request are separate tasks.

Who can apply

OESC assesses covered earnings, unemployment circumstances and continued eligibility. Follow the work-search and availability requirements assigned to your claim.

What help is available

Eligible weeks are paid to the program debit card. After activation, the vendor provides an option to transfer funds automatically to a personal bank account.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and address evidence.
  • Employer details and separation information.
  • Weekly earnings and work-search records.

How to get started

  1. Open the claimant portal

    Follow OESC’s Get Started link and complete the initial claim.

  2. Complete verification

    Respond to identity and eligibility requests and keep account contact details accurate.

  3. File weekly certifications

    Report earnings and other weekly circumstances. Record job-search activity as instructed.

After applying

Monitor portal notices. OESC identifies EmployOklahoma’s Job Search Activity tool for recording searches and lists 405-525-1500 for claim help.

Common questions

Can I receive payments without weekly filing?

Weekly certifications are required for the weeks requested.

Where can I save work-search records?

OESC recommends the Job Search Activity area in EmployOklahoma.

Does OESC directly deposit into my bank?

The state describes payment to its vendor debit card, with optional automatic transfers after activation.
