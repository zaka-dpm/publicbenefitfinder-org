Wisconsin Home Energy Assistance Program (WHEAP)

Wisconsin WHEAP helps eligible households with heating and electricity costs. The regular payment season runs October 1–May 15; outside that period, applications may be considered for other available assistance.

Who can apply

['Wisconsin residential households meeting income and program requirements.', 'The 2026–27 state income guidance uses 60% of Wisconsin median income; household size matters.']

What help is available

A one-time regular payment covering part of energy costs, plus potential crisis or related assistance depending on eligibility and funding.

Documents to prepare

Prepare household birth dates, all adult income sources, fuel type, supplier and account numbers, and landlord contact if renting. The checklist requests income for the month before applying.

How to get started

  1. Apply at energybenefit.wi.gov using a current email or mobile number to receive a passcode.
  2. Complete the application and send relevant requested proof.
  3. If disconnected, without heat or electricity, or facing disconnection, immediately call 866-432-8947 instead of relying on routine online review.

After applying

The local agency may request more information. Respond promptly; the portal says incomplete applications are denied after 30 days. Save a receipt and ask the supplier about arrangements while assistance is pending.

Common questions

Can I apply all year?

Yes; outside the heating season, other assistance is considered if funding is available.

Does regular help cover all energy costs?

The portal describes a contribution toward costs.

What if my online passcode expires?

The portal lets you request a new one; initial passcodes last 24 hours.

What income period should I prepare?

The state checklist lists the month before the application month.

Can I wait for routine review if my heat is off?

Call 866-432-8947 immediately for the urgent situation.
