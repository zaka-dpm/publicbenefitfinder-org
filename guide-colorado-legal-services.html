Colorado Legal Services

Colorado Legal Services offers civil legal help to eligible low-income residents and seniors. Its intake team checks the legal issue and available resources.

Who can apply

CLS handles civil matters rather than criminal or traffic cases. Eligibility, case priorities and staff capacity affect the help offered.

What help is available

Assistance may include advice or representation for housing, public benefits, consumer or family issues. Some courthouses offer eviction clinics.

Documents to prepare

  • Notices, summons and court papers, including all deadlines.
  • Income and household information for intake.
  • Relevant leases, benefit decisions, payment records or correspondence.

How to get started

  1. Choose an intake method

    Use Get Help to find the office serving your county, or start the linked online pre-application.

  2. Complete the screening

    A staff member must speak with you about eligibility and whether resources are available. State any hearing or response deadline immediately.

  3. Ask about an eviction clinic

    Check the listed courthouses and clinic schedule. Bring your summons and case documents; clinic assistance depends on eligibility and availability.

After applying

An online pre-application does not mean a lawyer has accepted your case. Keep court deadlines while waiting and ask what you should do next.

Common questions

Is this for criminal cases?

No. CLS describes its service as civil legal assistance, excluding criminal and traffic matters.

Is the online form the whole intake?

No. A staff member must follow up about eligibility and resources.

Can I attend any eviction clinic?

Check the location for the courthouse on your summons and the current schedule.

What if English is not my first language?

The Get Help page says staff can assist callers or visitors in their native language.
