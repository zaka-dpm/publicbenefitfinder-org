How it works

We do the searching across federal, state and local programs. You apply directly with the agency that runs each one.

  1. Choose what you need help with

    One click. No account and no Social Security number.

  2. Review programs that may fit your situation

    Guides cover eligibility rules, documents and application steps.

  3. Apply through the program agency

    Check the agency website for application steps and current requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What kinds of help can I find here?

Browse guides to food benefits, housing, utility bills, healthcare and other assistance. The directory covers 12 types of help, with programs organized by state.

Does Public Benefit Finder cost anything?

No. You can read our guides and use the finder for free, without creating an account. We do not charge to connect you with program information.

How do I find programs for my situation?

Use Find Help to choose your state and the support you need. ZIP code is optional. You can also browse by state or program. Guides explain application steps and link to the agency responsible; a finder result does not confirm eligibility.

Is this a government website?

No. Public Benefit Finder is an independent information site. We do not process applications or decide eligibility. Apply through the program agency and check its current requirements before submitting an application.

Facing a shutoff or eviction this week?

Call 211 today. Local agencies run emergency funds that can move within days, but they run out.
