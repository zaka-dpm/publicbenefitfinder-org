Aged, Blind, or Disabled (ABD) Cash Assistance

Washington ABD provides cash and disability-application support to eligible adults. DSHS conducts financial and medical assessments.

Who can apply

Applicants must live in Washington, meet income, resource and status rules, and be 65 or older, blind, or an adult with a condition preventing work for at least 12 consecutive months.

What help is available

Assistance includes a monthly grant, an HEN referral and help pursuing SSI or SSDI.

Documents to prepare

  • Income, resources and identity records.
  • Medical evidence requested by the social worker.

How to get started

  1. Apply

    Use Washington Connection, a Community Services Office or 877-501-2233.

  2. Complete the interview

    Financial interviews are available by phone or in person, weekdays 8 a.m.–2 p.m.

  3. Complete medical review

    If financially eligible, work with the assigned social worker.

After applying

Follow SSI application and case-management requests. ABD is not available to someone whose spouse or self already receives SSI.

Common questions

Does ABD approve federal disability benefits?

No. It helps with the separate federal application.

Is medical assessment required?

Disability applicants undergo medical eligibility review.

Does HEN referral guarantee housing?

Ask the HEN provider about available support.

Where can I apply offline?

Contact a Community Services Office or phone intake.
