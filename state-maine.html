Assistance programs in Maine

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Maine SNAP (Food Supplement)

    Maine SNAP provides monthly food benefits through EBT. Apply through My Maine Connection or the Office for Family Independence, then complete the required interview.

    Read the application guide

  • Maine WIC Nutrition Program

    Maine WIC provides food benefits, nutrition support and breastfeeding help. Parents and caregivers can apply for eligible infants and children in their care.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Home Energy Assistance Program (HEAP / LIHEAP)

    Maine HEAP helps eligible homeowners and renters with heating costs. The 2026–27 season began August 3, 2026 and runs until funds are exhausted or May 29, 2027.

    Read the application guide

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (Maine)

    Maine weatherization provides approved home efficiency improvements at no cost to eligible households. Local community action agencies arrange the assessment and work.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • MaineCare (Maine Medicaid)

    MaineCare is Maine’s Medicaid coverage. The Office for Family Independence decides eligibility, while the Office of MaineCare Services handles covered services and billing.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • General Assistance (Maine)

    Maine General Assistance helps people who lack the income or resources to meet basic needs. Applications are handled by the local municipal office.
