DC Energy Assistance (LIHEAP & Utility Discount Program)

DC offers energy-bill assistance through LIHEAP and discounts through UDP. DOEE announces FY27 applications opening October 20, 2026.

  1. Who can apply

Who can apply

LIHEAP assesses household income and energy circumstances. UDP has its own income rules and requires bills in the applicant’s name. Do not assume one approval automatically grants both.

What help is available

LIHEAP can help with heating or cooling costs; UDP reduces qualifying utility charges. Emergency support has additional conditions.

Documents to prepare

  • Photo ID and requested household Social Security records.
  • Income evidence and recent utility bills.
  • Any disconnection notice or utility confirmation of disconnected service.

How to get started

  1. Check the opening notice

    Review LIHEAP and UDP for October 20 application instructions.

  2. Ask DOEE about urgency

    Call 311 or 202-737-4404 if service is disconnected or fuel is depleted.

  3. Submit directly to DOEE

    Use DOEE’s current authorized application route. The LIHEAP page warns against submitting through unauthorized third parties.

After applying

Save the confirmation and contact the utility about pending disconnection. Previous-year dollar tables should not be treated as FY27 limits.

Common questions

When do FY27 applications open?

DOEE announces October 20, 2026 for both programs.

Are LIHEAP and UDP the same?

No. One provides assistance; the other offers utility discounts.

Must UDP bills be in my name?

Yes, according to the current overview.

Can I give LIHEAP papers to a third party?

DOEE says applications must be submitted directly to it.
