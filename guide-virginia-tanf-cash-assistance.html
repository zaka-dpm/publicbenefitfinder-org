Virginia TANF (Cash Assistance)

Virginia TANF offers temporary monthly support to eligible families with children. Local DSS reviews the household and its participation requirements.

Who can apply

Income, resources, child and family circumstances and applicable status rules affect eligibility. Ask the worker about exceptions or assistance routes for your situation.

What help is available

Cash support helps meet basic family needs. VIEW and related services can assist with employment and participation barriers.

Documents to prepare

  • Household identity and child information.
  • Income and resource records.
  • Relevant school, child care or participation-barrier evidence.

How to get started

  1. Apply

    Use CommonHelp, call 855-635-4370 or submit the paper application to local DSS.

  2. Complete assessment

    Return requested records and discuss the family’s needs.

  3. Review the plan

    Ask about VIEW duties, exemptions and related support before missing an activity.

After applying

Report changes and follow the notice. For a one-time crisis, ask DSS whether Diversionary or Emergency Assistance fits the case.

Common questions

Is TANF unlimited support?

It is temporary assistance with program limits.

Can I apply on paper?

Return the state form to local DSS.

What if transport prevents participation?

Discuss the barrier with the worker and ask about support.

Is one-time crisis support identical to monthly TANF?

No. Ask about the separate assistance routes.
