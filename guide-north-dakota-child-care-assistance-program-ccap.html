North Dakota Child Care Assistance Program (CCAP)

North Dakota CCAP helps qualifying families with child-care costs. New applications can be placed on a waiting list, so confirm the current position before arranging care on the assumption of a subsidy.

Who can apply

The state reviews income, resources, the need for care and provider requirements. Age and attendance rules depend on the child and provider arrangement.

What help is available

Approved payments help with eligible care, but a family may owe a balance above the program payment.

Documents to prepare

  • Child identity or birth records.
  • Income and requested asset information.
  • Work or other care-need records and provider details.

How to get started

  1. Apply through the state

    Use CCAP’s portal or paper route.

  2. Confirm the provider and child rules

    Ask how the April 2026 attendance and age changes affect your arrangement, including special-needs or tribal care.

  3. Follow waiting-list instructions

    Keep your contact information current and respond if funding becomes available.

After applying

Check the authorization dates and family share before care begins. The state says waiting-list time is not backdated for payment.

Common questions

Is a child’s Social Security number required?

The state describes it as optional and says withholding it does not affect participation.

Does every applicant wait?

The state identifies exceptions including TANF and Crossroads participants, with priority arrangements for qualifying homeless families.

Does approval always pay the entire provider charge?

No. Families can owe charges beyond the approved subsidy.
