Kentucky 211

Kentucky 211 connects residents with local food, housing, utility, health and other community services. The statewide county map also helps you find the directory serving your area.

Who can apply

['Anyone can request a referral without first qualifying for a government benefit.', 'Each referred organization decides whether it can serve your location and circumstances.']

What help is available

Specialists provide resource information around the clock. A referral is a starting point for contacting a provider; it does not award money or reserve an appointment.

Documents to prepare

Keep your county and ZIP code handy. Describe the immediate problem, deadlines and providers you have already tried. These details help narrow a search and are preparation tips, not proof required for a 211 application.

How to get started

  1. Call 211 or text your ZIP code to 898211.
  2. For online searching, open Kentucky 211 and select your county on the resource map.
  3. Ask for the provider’s contact information, service area and intake method. Contact that provider to request its service.

After applying

Record the referral and the provider’s response. If it cannot help, ask 211 for alternatives and explain why the first referral did not work. Contact emergency services for an immediate danger rather than waiting for a resource search.

Common questions

Can I call outside business hours?

Kentucky 211 advertises telephone information and referral 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Can I search by county?

Yes. The Kentucky 211 website has a county map linking to regional resource directories.

Does 211 decide benefit eligibility?

No. The agency offering the service conducts its own intake.

What should I mention about a utility problem?

Explain whether service is disconnected or scheduled for disconnection and give the date. Ask the referred provider whether it is currently accepting applications.
