Assistance programs in Ohio

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Food assistance

  • Ohio WIC

    Read the application guide

  • SNAP Food Assistance (Ohio)

    Ohio SNAP helps eligible households buy groceries using an Ohio Direction EBT card. County JFS determines the household’s benefit.

    Read the application guide

Housing assistance

  • Ohio Legal Help (Eviction & Legal Aid)

    Ohio Legal Help offers legal information, forms and connections to local assistance. Start promptly if a notice or court paper has a deadline.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • HEAP Energy Assistance (Ohio)

    Ohio HEAP helps eligible households cover part of their home energy costs. A separate crisis request may be needed for an urgent shutoff.

    Read the application guide

  • Home Weatherization Assistance Program (Ohio HWAP)

    Ohio HWAP provides home energy improvements to eligible households at no cost. A home inspection determines suitable work.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • Medicaid (Ohio)

    Ohio Medicaid provides health coverage for eligible residents. County Job and Family Services reviews the application and requested evidence.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • PIPP Plus (Percentage of Income Payment Plan)

    PIPP Plus helps eligible Ohio utility customers manage monthly electric and natural-gas payments according to household income.

    Read the application guide

  • Ohio Earned Income Credit

    Ohio’s earned income credit reduces state income tax for eligible taxpayers who qualify for federal EITC.

    Read the application guide

  • Ohio Works First (Cash Assistance)

    Ohio Works First offers temporary cash support to eligible families while adults prepare for employment. Your county JFS assesses the request.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Benefits (Ohio)

    Ohio unemployment compensation provides temporary payments to eligible workers after qualifying unemployment. The state decides monetary and weekly eligibility separately.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • Ohio 211

    Ohio 211 connects residents with local food, housing, utility and other community services.

    Read the application guide
