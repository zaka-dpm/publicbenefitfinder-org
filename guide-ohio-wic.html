Ohio WIC

Ohio WIC supplies nutrition support for eligible pregnant, postpartum and breastfeeding participants, infants and children younger than five. Enrollment happens through a local clinic.

Who can apply

The clinic checks residence, income and nutritional risk. Columbus Public Health lists the Ohio income guideline as 185% of federal poverty guidelines.

What help is available

Services include selected nutritious foods, nutrition counseling, breastfeeding support and referrals. WIC is separate from SNAP.

Documents to prepare

  • Identification, birth-date and address evidence.
  • Income records and any Medicaid or SNAP information.
  • Pregnancy documentation when requested; bring participating children as instructed.

How to get started

  1. Find a clinic

    Contact the WIC clinic serving your county. Franklin County residents can use Columbus Public Health WIC or call 614-645-7280.

  2. Arrange certification

    Ask what to bring and who must attend.

  3. Complete assessment

    The clinic reviews eligibility and explains food benefits and follow-up visits.

After applying

Use benefits within their assigned period and keep clinic appointments. Confirm local procedures rather than assuming statewide walk-in intake.

Common questions

Is the Columbus phone statewide intake?

No. It serves Franklin County; use your local clinic elsewhere.

Does WIC cover every grocery item?

No. Benefits cover the assigned food package.

Can SNAP recipients ask about WIC?

Yes. Bring benefit information for the clinic to review.

Can I just walk in?

Call the clinic first; Columbus currently requires appointments.
