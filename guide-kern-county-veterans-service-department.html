Kern County Veterans Service Department

Kern County Veterans Service is a local point of contact for veterans seeking help with benefits and service questions. Contact the department to arrange an appointment.

Who can apply

['Veterans across Kern County can contact the department. Eligibility for a particular VA or other benefit is determined by that benefit’s rules.']

What help is available

The office can be contacted for veteran service inquiries. Ask whether your issue requires claim assistance, a referral or contact with the agency administering the benefit.

Documents to prepare

When booking, ask what service records, decision notices or other documents to bring. Make a short list of questions and explain any claim deadline before the appointment.

How to get started

Call 661-868-7300 or email kernvets@kerncounty.com. The current contact page lists appointments at 1120 Golden State Avenue, Bakersfield.

After applying

Keep copies of any paperwork submitted and confirm which agency will respond. Ask how to track a claim and who to contact if a notice arrives before the next appointment.

Common questions

Should I arrive without an appointment?

The current contact page describes visits by appointment; contact the office first.

Does contacting the county guarantee VA approval?

No. The benefit agency makes its own decision.

Where is the Bakersfield office?

1120 Golden State Avenue, according to the current department contact page.

What should I bring?

Ask staff for the checklist for your particular issue when scheduling.
