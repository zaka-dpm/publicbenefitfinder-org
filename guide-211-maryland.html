211 Maryland

211 Maryland helps residents find local health and human services, including food, housing and utility resources. Specialists use your location and circumstances to narrow the search.

Who can apply

['The free, confidential service is available to everyone in Maryland.', 'Eligibility and funds are assessed separately by each referred provider.']

What help is available

Telephone specialists are available 24 hours a day, every day. The online database offers another way to locate services. A referral does not reserve money or approve a benefit.

Documents to prepare

Have your ZIP code, immediate need and any deadline ready. Note providers you have tried and why they could not help. These are preparation suggestions, not an application checklist.

How to get started

  1. Call 211.
  2. Alternatively search the resource database linked at 211 Maryland.
  3. Contact the referred organization to confirm service area, intake and current availability.

After applying

Record referrals and responses. Ask for another option if the first provider cannot serve you. Confirm whether a listed text service provides resource conversation or an ongoing information subscription before relying on it for urgent help.

Common questions

Can I call at night?

Yes. Maryland’s telephone specialists are available around the clock.

Does 211 require a low-income application?

No. Anyone in Maryland can ask for resource information.

Can I look up services myself?

Yes. Use the linked online database.

Does a referral approve assistance?

No. Contact the provider for its own eligibility review.
