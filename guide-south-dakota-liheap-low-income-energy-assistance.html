South Dakota LIHEAP (Low Income Energy Assistance)

South Dakota energy assistance accepts online or paper applications. Make sure the household application has the required adult signatures.

Who can apply

The state reviews income, household size and responsibility for heating costs.

What help is available

Approved heating payments go to the energy supplier.

Documents to prepare

  • Prior three calendar months of household income.
  • Heating and electric bills or landlord heating statement.
  • Required adult signatures.

How to get started

  1. Apply through DSS

    Use the energy application links.

  2. Provide records

    Submit income and heating evidence using the agency instructions.

  3. Flag a crisis

    Call 800-233-8503 about a disconnect notice or fuel shortage.

After applying

Watch mailed decisions and document requests; use the agency status option if needed.

Common questions

Who receives the payment?

The state pays the energy supplier.

What if heat is in my rent?

The checklist accepts a landlord statement explaining heating charges.

Should I mention a disconnect notice?

Yes. Contact the energy office about crisis screening.
