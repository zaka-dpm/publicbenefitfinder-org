Temporary Assistance (Family Assistance & Safety Net)

New York Temporary Assistance includes Family Assistance and Safety Net Assistance. Local districts assess regular and emergency requests.

  1. Who can apply

Who can apply

Financial, household and program conditions apply. Work activities or other cooperation requirements may affect a case.

What help is available

Assistance can support basic needs; emergency help may address qualifying urgent costs. The district determines the form and amount.

Documents to prepare

  • Identity and household records.
  • Income and shelter-cost evidence.
  • Emergency notices and requested eligibility records.

How to get started

  1. Choose local intake

    Use myBenefits or contact the district; NYC applicants use ACCESS HRA.

  2. Explain emergencies

    State the immediate need and deadlines to a worker.

  3. Complete the interview

    Follow local document and activity instructions.

After applying

Read decisions and hearing instructions. Ask about exceptions or safety concerns with required activities.

Common questions

Is screening the same as applying?

No. File an application.

Can I request one-time emergency help?

Ask the district about the relevant emergency category.

Does NYC use the same portal?

NYC uses ACCESS HRA.

Who decides eligibility?

The local social-services district.
