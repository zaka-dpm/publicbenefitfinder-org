About Public Benefit Finder

We help people find assistance programs they may qualify for, explained in plain language, and send them to the official application. It is 100% free.

Why we exist

Help with food, rent and bills is out there, but it is spread across hundreds of agencies, each with its own names, forms and rules. We bring it together in one place and explain it the way a helpful caseworker would.

How we write our guides

  • Every guide is written from the official program rules and agency sites.
  • Each guide names the agency that runs the program and links to its application.
  • We show the date we last checked each guide, and update guides when rules change.

What we are not

Public Benefit Finder is an independent guide. We are not a government agency, we don't take applications and we don't decide who qualifies. Only the agency that runs a program can do that.

You should never pay to apply

Applying for public benefits is free. If anyone asks for a fee to apply or to speed up your application, it is not the official program.
