Idaho SNAP (Food Stamps)

Idaho SNAP helps qualifying households buy eligible groceries. Apply through DHW and complete its interview; Idaho’s current food-purchase restrictions differ from older national summaries.

Who can apply

DHW reviews Idaho residence, household income and resources, status rules and applicable work requirements or exceptions. A household cannot receive SNAP from two states at once; tell DHW if you recently moved.

What help is available

Approved households use EBT for qualifying food purchases. Since February 15, 2026, Idaho SNAP excludes candy and soda under its state restriction; use the agency’s current product guidance for details.

Documents to prepare

  • Identification and household income or resource information.
  • Housing costs and current monthly expenses.
  • Applicable status records and information about benefits in another state.

How to get started

  1. Choose a DHW application route

    Use the SNAP application page. It lists phone at 877-456-1233, a field office and a downloaded form submitted by mail, email or fax.

  2. Complete the interview

    DHW contacts you after receiving and reviewing the application. Explain household circumstances and provide the requested verification.

  3. Read the notice

    Keep the eligibility decision and follow EBT-card and renewal instructions.

After applying

Use the current Idaho SNAP customer updates when planning purchases. Report changes and complete reviews as directed by DHW.

Common questions

Is an interview required?

Idaho’s application page says an interview is required along with the application.

Can candy or soda be bought with Idaho SNAP?

Idaho’s restriction took effect February 15, 2026. The agency provides product definitions and checkout guidance.

What if I moved from another state?

Tell DHW about the other state’s case. SNAP cannot be received from two states at once.
