Utah Legal Services & Timpanogos Legal Center

Utah Legal Services and Timpanogos Legal Center provide different civil legal-help routes. Ask the provider which service fits your case before assuming full representation.

Who can apply

Each organization screens its own cases. Timpanogos clinics assess income or an abuse-related qualifying situation.

What help is available

Legal information, brief advice, clinics or qualifying case assistance depending on provider and case.

Documents to prepare

  • Court notices and deadlines.
  • Relevant agreements and correspondence.
  • Income information requested during screening.

How to get started

  1. Choose the relevant service

    Review Utah Legal Services topics and Timpanogos programs.

  2. Request screening

    Explain your location, issue and upcoming court date.

  3. Confirm the scope

    Ask whether the provider offers advice, documents or representation.

After applying

Follow court notices while waiting and keep copies of documents given to intake.

Common questions

Do all clinics prepare every type of filing?

Timpanogos describes its document clinic as certain family-law filings.

Can housing issues fit a clinic?

Timpanogos lists housing among legal-advice clinic topics.

Is interpretation available?

Utah Legal Services advertises free interpreters; ask intake about your language.
