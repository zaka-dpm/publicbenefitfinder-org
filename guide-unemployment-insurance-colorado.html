Unemployment Insurance (Colorado)

Colorado unemployment insurance provides temporary wage replacement for qualifying workers. MyUI+ handles claims, follow-up tasks and payment requests.

Who can apply

CDLE reviews recent wages and the reason work ended or hours were reduced. Ongoing eligibility also matters. A wage-screening threshold alone does not guarantee benefits.

What help is available

A approved claim provides temporary payments while you meet the program’s conditions. The amount and duration are determined from your claim.

Documents to prepare

  • Employer names, addresses, employment dates and pay information for the past 18 months.
  • Pay stubs and information about how work ended.
  • Identity documents needed for ID.me and any records CDLE requests.

How to get started

  1. Start or reopen the claim

    Use the MyUI+ link on CDLE’s Start a Claim page. Returning claimants should use their existing account.

  2. Verify identity

    Complete each ID.me request CDLE sends; the state may request verification more than once.

  3. Handle required tasks

    Check the claim tracker, upload requested documents and follow the claimant guide for weekly payment and work-search responsibilities.

After applying

Save upload confirmations and check determinations. If the system reports an unfamiliar existing account, contact CDLE rather than creating repeated new accounts.

Common questions

I claimed before. Do I create a new account?

CDLE says claimants who filed since 2015 are returning claimants; use the registration instructions for the existing account.

Why am I asked to verify identity again?

CDLE says it may require repeated ID.me verification for claim actions.

How do I send additional records?

Use MyUI+ and the issue identified in your claim tracker, following CDLE’s upload instructions.

What if an account exists that I did not create?

Contact CDLE customer support at 303-318-9000 or 800-388-5515.
