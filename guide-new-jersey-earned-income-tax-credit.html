New Jersey Earned Income Tax Credit

New Jersey’s earned income tax credit is claimed through the state income-tax return. Use instructions for the year being filed rather than treating the credit as a benefit-portal application.

Who can apply

Resident workers must meet the federal EITC criteria or the state’s special age provision. For 2025, New Jersey also considers qualifying applicants aged 18 or older who meet federal criteria except the federal age limit.

What help is available

For tax year 2025, the state credit is 40% of the federal EITC calculation. A part-year resident’s credit is adjusted for the months of New Jersey residence.

Documents to prepare

  • Federal return and EITC calculation.
  • Wage and other income statements.
  • Dependent and Social Security information.
  • New Jersey residence dates if you moved during the year.

How to get started

  1. Calculate the year’s credit

    Follow New Jersey Taxation’s EITC instructions.

  2. Claim it on the state return

    Use the correct NJ return year and the special instructions if federal age rules prevented a federal award.

  3. File and retain evidence

    Keep the calculation, filed returns and submission confirmation.

After applying

Read any Taxation notice. If the federal EITC changes after filing, ask a tax preparer how it affects the state credit and whether amendment is needed.

Common questions

Do I need children to qualify?

Not necessarily. The state includes qualifying workers without children, subject to its criteria.

Is 40% a rule for every past year?

This article’s figure is specifically verified for tax year 2025; use the form for the year filed.

What if I lived in NJ for only part of 2025?

The state notice describes a residence-month adjustment; follow the tax instructions.
