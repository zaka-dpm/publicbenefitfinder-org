Weatherization Assistance Program (Hawaii)

Hawaiʻi weatherization applications belong with the agency serving your county. HCAP is the Honolulu provider; residents of other islands should use the state’s county-agency directory.

Who can apply

The program serves eligible low-income households and prioritizes specified households, including older people, people with disabilities, families with children and homes with high energy burdens. The provider checks eligibility and the home’s suitability.

What help is available

An assessment identifies appropriate efficiency measures and energy-conservation advice. Possible measures include lighting, water-saving fixtures and selected efficient equipment; the home assessment determines the approved work.

Documents to prepare

  • Prepare income and household information.
  • Have your utility records and address available.
  • Ask about ownership or tenancy records and any required owner permission.

How to get started

  1. Find the county provider

    Use the Office of Community Services directory for Honolulu, Hawaiʻi, Maui or Kauaʻi.

  2. Request intake

    Honolulu residents can contact HCAP’s weatherization program. Confirm application availability and the provider’s checklist.

  3. Arrange the home assessment

    Explain energy problems and follow the provider’s audit and scheduling instructions.

After applying

Confirm the approved scope before work starts. Ask the provider about timing, access and any safety issue discovered during assessment.

Common questions

Does HCAP serve every island?

The state lists separate county agencies. Use the provider serving your county.

Is weatherization a cash grant?

It provides selected efficiency services and education, rather than an unrestricted cash payment.

Will all appliances be replaced?

No automatic replacement is promised. Measures depend on eligibility, the assessment and program approval.
