Unemployment Insurance (Kansas)

Kansas unemployment insurance provides temporary benefits to eligible workers. Apply and submit separate weekly certifications through KansasUI.gov.

Who can apply

KDOL checks covered wages, the employment-separation reason and weekly ability and availability for work. Reduced hours may qualify. Quitting or discharge requires review rather than an automatic assumption.

What help is available

The agency determines the weekly and maximum benefit from wages and the claim. A benefits card by itself does not establish eligibility.

Documents to prepare

Keep employer details, employment dates, pay records and separation information ready. Record work-search activities and any gross wages or other payments for weekly certification.

How to get started

  1. Create an account at KansasUI.gov and submit the initial claim. Follow identity and evidence requests.

  2. Continue weekly certifications while unemployed, including the waiting week and while a decision is pending. Complete required work-search activities unless exempt and report earnings accurately.

After applying

Read the approval or denial notice. If weekly filing fails, contact KDOL immediately; its current handbook lists 800-292-6333. Use the decision’s reconsideration or appeal instructions.

Common questions

Does the waiting week require certification?

Yes. KDOL says certification is needed for it to count.

Can reduced hours qualify?

Potentially; KDOL reviews wages and weekly circumstances.

Should I stop weekly claims while awaiting a decision?

KDOL directs claimants to continue weekly certifications while unemployed.

Does receiving a card mean I was approved?

No. Check the eligibility decision and claim status.
