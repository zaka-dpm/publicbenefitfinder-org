TANF Cash Assistance

Pennsylvania TANF helps eligible pregnant participants and families caring for dependent children meet basic needs.

Who can apply

The county assistance office checks income, resources, residence, status and family relationships. Child and work-participation rules apply, with case-specific exceptions.

What help is available

Temporary cash support is available under program rules. The state describes a usual lifetime limit of 60 months.

Documents to prepare

  • Family and child relationship information.
  • Income and resource evidence.
  • Requested identity, status and Social Security records.

How to get started

  1. Apply

    Use COMPASS or return the state application to your county assistance office.

  2. Discuss a plan

    Complete the Agreement of Mutual Responsibility with your caseworker, including any participation barriers.

  3. Review the notice

    Check the decision, duties and application status at trackmybenefits.pa.gov.

After applying

Discuss work, child care, transport or health changes with the worker. Ask about related support when cash assistance ends.

Common questions

Can a relative caregiver apply?

The program includes eligible relatives caring for dependent children.

Is support unlimited?

The state lists a usual lifetime limit of five years.

What is the AMR?

It records the plan and responsibilities agreed with the caseworker.

What if disability affects participation?

Tell the worker and ask about applicable exceptions or support.
