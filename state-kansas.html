Assistance programs in Kansas

Explore public benefits and assistance resources by the kind of help you need. Agencies decide eligibility and availability.

Find programs for your situation

Food assistance

  • Kansas Food Assistance (SNAP)

    Kansas Food Assistance is the state’s SNAP program. DCF screens household applications and loads approved monthly benefits onto the Kansas Benefits Card.

    Read the application guide

  • Kansas WIC

    Kansas WIC provides approved foods and nutrition support through local clinics. A new applicant can start with the state’s online pre-application.

    Read the application guide

Utility assistance

  • Weatherization Assistance Program (Kansas)

    Kansas weatherization provides approved home energy improvements at no charge to eligible households. Apply through the provider serving your county.

    Read the application guide

  • Kansas LIEAP (Low Income Energy Assistance)

    Kansas LIEAP provides a one-time annual heating benefit to eligible households. The 2026 application period has ended; DCF has not yet announced the next opening.

    Read the application guide

Health coverage

  • KanCare (Kansas Medicaid & CHIP)

    KanCare is Kansas’s Medicaid and CHIP coverage system. Use the medical application portal and the form appropriate to your household.

    Read the application guide

Cash and income support

  • Kansas Earned Income Tax Credit

    Kansas’s earned income tax credit is claimed on the state income-tax return and is based on the federal EITC.

    Read the application guide

  • Kansas TANF Cash Assistance (Successful Families Program)

    Kansas’s Successful Families Program combines TANF cash assistance with employment and support services for eligible families raising children.

    Read the application guide

  • Unemployment Insurance (Kansas)

    Kansas unemployment insurance provides temporary benefits to eligible workers. Apply and submit separate weekly certifications through KansasUI.gov.

    Read the application guide

Child care assistance

  • Kansas Child Care Assistance

    Kansas Child Care Assistance helps eligible families pay part of approved childcare costs. The family must use a DCF-enrolled provider.

    Read the application guide

Additional support

  • Kansas 211

    Kansas 211 provides free, confidential referrals to local food, housing, health and other services. Live specialists have weekday hours.

    Read the application guide

  • Kansas Legal Services

    Kansas Legal Services screens requests for civil legal help by telephone and an online intake process. State any court deadline when applying.

    Read the application guide

Program names come from our existing directory. Funding and rules can change; confirm details with the administering agency before applying.
