Kansas LIEAP (Low Income Energy Assistance)

Kansas LIEAP provides a one-time annual heating benefit to eligible households. The 2026 application period has ended; DCF has not yet announced the next opening.

Who can apply

An adult at the address must be responsible for heating costs paid to a landlord, utility or fuel supplier. DCF reviews combined household income and other conditions during the application period.

What help is available

The benefit pays part of home heating costs, including eligible electricity, gas, propane or other heating fuels.

Documents to prepare

Use the season’s checklist for income and heating bills. DCF requires signatures from all adults living in the residence.

How to get started

  1. Check DCF’s LIEAP page for the next announced application window. Do not use an old closing date as a current deadline.

  2. When applications reopen, use the DCF portal or request a paper form at the local Service Center. For immediate utility trouble, contact the supplier and ask about payment arrangements or other local aid.

After applying

Read the decision and verify how assistance is credited. Ask the utility about Cold Weather Rule conditions rather than assuming protection happens automatically.

Common questions

Can I apply now in October 2026?

DCF says the 2026 period ended and the next opening will be announced.

Can heating paid through rent qualify?

The eligibility page includes responsibility for heating costs payable to a landlord.

Is the benefit paid monthly?

DCF describes a one-time annual benefit.

Does it cover the entire energy bill?

No. It helps with a portion.
